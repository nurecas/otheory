# Esoteric domain — survey overview

## Scope of this pass
Breadth-first (SURVEY phase) inventory of sacred geometry, Hermeticism, subtle-body/energy systems,
astrology, alchemy, Kabbalah, gematria/numerology, Theosophy, "morphic resonance," and ley-line/frequency
pseudoscience. `golden-ratio-ubiquity` (E6) and `platonic-solids-five` (E1) were seeded prior to this pass
and are not duplicated here; this survey covers the wider field around them.

## The recurring split: symbol vs. science-claim
Nearly every system in this domain has two honest readings that must not be collapsed into one claim:

1. **Symbol/practice (E5)** — a real, historically attested contemplative, artistic, or interpretive
   tradition, represented as that tradition's own claim. Not a demerit; an honest classification.
2. **Failed-science overreach (E6)** — the same material asserted as a literal physical mechanism,
   measurable force, or predictive science. This fails ordinary vetting: no detected field, no controlled
   confirming study, or a falsified prediction.

Where both readings are actively made (in practice or in popular culture), this survey files **two
separate claims** rather than one claim carrying both registers. Where the split is more a matter of
labeling than of two live camps, one claim states the split explicitly.

Pairs filed this way in this pass:
- Sacred geometry: `sacred-geometry-symbolic-practice` (E5) vs. `sacred-geometry-cosmic-blueprint-claim` (E6).
- Chakras/subtle body: `chakra-nadi-contemplative-map` (E5) vs. `chakra-energy-measurable-claim` (E6).

Single claims that state the split internally: `alchemy-symbolic-protochemistry` (E5 for the documented
lab practice and symbolism; explicitly notes the transmutation goal was tested and failed),
`gematria-numerology-practice` (E5 for exegetical practice; notes predictive numerology would be E6).

## Historical corrections worth flagging to the synthesizer
- **The Kybalion is not ancient.** It is a 1908 New Thought book by "Three Initiates" (widely attributed
  to William Walker Atkinson), inventing a fake pedigree (the word "Kybalion" has no attested prior use).
  Its "Seven Hermetic Principles," including "vibration," draw substantially on 19th-century New Thought
  and philosophers like David Hartley — not on the Corpus Hermeticum. The genuine ancient Hermetic corpus
  is the Corpus Hermeticum (17 Greek treatises, c. 100-300 CE), and it should be the citation used whenever
  "Hermetic" claims are invoked, with the Kybalion flagged as a modern popularization if referenced at all.
- **Theosophy (1875) is a modern synthesis**, not a transmission of ancient hidden teaching, per its own
  historical record (Blavatsky's sourcing from contemporary Orientalist scholarship). It is nonetheless
  historically important as the direct ancestor of much later New Age "vibration/energy/planes" vocabulary.
- **Isaac Casaubon's 1614 philological demonstration** that the Corpus Hermeticum postdates Plato (written
  in Koine Greek, not a document contemporary with Moses) is itself a historical landmark, correcting the
  Renaissance belief in Hermes Trismegistus's extreme antiquity.
- **The Zohar's authorship** is dated by Gershom Scholem's philology substantially to Moses de Leon
  (c. 1280-1286 CE), not to the 2nd-century sage Shimon bar Yochai to whom tradition attributes it — a
  parallel case of a text's claimed antiquity outrunning its actual composition date.

## Domain-level tensions spotted (for the synthesizer)
1. **Antiquity is repeatedly used as a rhetorical stand-in for evidence** — the Kybalion, Theosophy, and
   some "ancient sacred geometry" claims all lean on a false or exaggerated pedigree to imply authority.
   CLAUDE.md's instruction that "popularity or antiquity is not evidence" is directly tested by this domain
   more than almost any other.
2. **The same word ("energy," "vibration," "frequency," "field") is used across chakras, ley lines,
   Sheldrake, and frequency-healing claims without a shared operational definition** — none of these
   "energies" has been measured by an instrument, and each borrows unearned credibility from the fact that
   physics does have real, measurable fields and vibrations. This is the single most important line for
   O Theory to hold: its own "resonance/subtle-energy" imagery must not imply support from any of these
   claims.
3. **Sheldrake sits at the boundary of E3/E6.** He is a credentialed biologist raising genuine open
   questions in developmental biology (the limits of gene-only explanations of form), but the specific
   morphic-resonance mechanism he proposes has a poor replication record and no independent physical
   detection — tiered E6 here rather than E3 because the mechanism itself (not just an open question it
   responds to) has been tested and has failed, repeatedly, including in independent replications
   unfavorable to the hypothesis (Wiseman). A vetter revisiting this could reasonably discuss whether a
   softer E3-with-heavy-caveats framing is more honest for the underlying open biological question, tiered
   separately from the morphic-resonance mechanism claim itself.
4. **Astrology's Carlson (1985) result is itself contested** (Ertel's 2009 reanalysis disputes Carlson's
   statistical treatment and argues the data, taken as a whole, shows an above-chance effect). This survey
   tiers astrology E6 on the mainstream reading and pre-registered criterion, but flags the dispute so a
   deep-dive would need to read both Carlson and Ertel directly rather than take either side's summary.
5. **Sacred geometry's E5/E6 split is a template** other cross-domain bridges may want to reuse: real
   Euclidean math (already E1 via `platonic-solids-five`) leads to honest symbolic use (E5) and separately
   to an unsupported "cosmic blueprint" claim (E6). The same three-layer pattern likely recurs for
   numerology and for frequency/vibration claims.

  secondary summaries of the dispute rather than reading both papers directly.
