## Scope of this pass

This survey covers the empirical and theoretical landscape of consciousness science not already filed under
the seed claims `iit-integrated-information`, `orch-or-microtubules`, and `llm-subjective-reports`. It adds
14 new claim files. Consistent with CLAUDE.md's consciousness-specific caution, the guiding method throughout
was: **file the observation and its interpretation as two separate claims whenever their tiers differ**, and
never let an observation's empirical credibility transfer to a metaphysical reading riding on it.

## The observation/interpretation splits (the core of this domain)

This is where O Theory's narrative most needs fencing. Four explicit splits were made this pass, each as two
files with a `depends_on` link from interpretation to observation:

1. **Terminal lucidity.** Observation (E3): caregivers/clinicians document real, non-trivial rates of
   unexpected lucid episodes in advanced dementia, now a funded NIA research target with a working typology
   (Griffin et al. 2024). Interpretation (E4): reading this as the brain's "filter" on a non-local mind
   temporarily loosening. The standard research programs pursuing this phenomenon (e.g., transient
   amyloid-oligomer/synaptic dynamics) are themselves materialist hypotheses, not filtration hypotheses — the
   filter reading is a further, separately-argued philosophical step with no distinguishing prediction of its
   own.

2. **Near-death experience phenomenology (AWARE-II).** Observation (E3): Parnia et al. 2023 found ~39% of a
   28-patient interviewed subsample recalled experiences during cardiac arrest, and documented delta/theta/
   alpha EEG activity re-emerging during CPR in a monitored subset, despite severe hypoxia. The single
   objective test embedded in the study (identifying an auditory stimulus played during arrest) returned a
   near-chance result (1/28) — a null finding for veridical perception during arrest that tempers the
   headline recall statistic considerably. Interpretation (E4): reading this as evidence of non-local or
   surviving consciousness. The study's own preferred mechanism (disinhibition of memory-retrieval systems)
   is itself materialist and unconfirmed, not a filtration/survival claim.

3. **Anesthesia and microtubules.** The existing seed claim `orch-or-microtubules` already covers the Orch-OR
   theoretical interpretation; this pass adds `wiest-anesthesia-microtubule-observation` (E3) as the
   observation-level claim — Meyer-Overton correlation, dose additivity, the epothilone-B/isoflurane rat
   result, and quantum-chemical potency modeling of tubulin binding. These are real, published, quantitative
   pharmacology results that do not by themselves require any quantum-consciousness account; Wiest's own
   paper concedes the further "macroscopic quantum entanglement" evidence (Kerskens & Pérez MRI claim) is
   contested and unresolved.

4. **COGITATE vs. the theories it tested.** The study (E1, as a well-designed preregistered experiment) is
   filed separately from GNWT (E3) and from the existing IIT claim (E3), because the study confirmed some
   predictions and disconfirmed others for BOTH theories, and the theory proponents (notably Naccache et al.
   for GNWT) dispute the interpretation of the disconfirmed predictions post-publication. Neither theory was
   vindicated; O Theory must not read COGITATE as crowning either framework.

Two further items were flagged as belonging to this same observation/interpretation logic but were left as
single claims because their interpretive half is either already covered elsewhere or not yet warranted as a
distinct filed claim: psychedelic predictive-processing models (materialist mechanism only, filed at E3;
any "psychedelics reveal a filtered non-local reality" reading is a further E4 step not separately filed this
pass) and LLM bliss-attractor behavior (already split in the seed claim `llm-subjective-reports`).

## Psi / non-local mind — replication record

Per the domain-specific instruction, psi claims are tiered honestly rather than as live minority science by
default:

- **Bem (2011) precognition/presentiment**: tiered **E6**, not E3, because three independent preregistered
  direct replications of the flagship effect returned a combined null result (one-tailed p = .83), and the
  original study is now a textbook case in the psychology replication-crisis literature motivating
  preregistration reforms. This is a stronger disconfirmation than most heterodox-but-live E3 claims in this
  corpus (e.g., Orch-OR) have received.
- **Ganzfeld telepathy**: tiered **E3 with heavy caveats** rather than E6, because unlike Bem, there is no
  clean, adversarially-preregistered multi-site null replication — instead there is an unresolved
  meta-analytic methodology dispute (Storm/Tressoldi/Di Risio 2010 vs. Hyman 2010) and a documented
  lab-dependent effect (works for some experimenters, not most), which is itself a red flag for uncontrolled
  variables rather than evidence of a robust physical phenomenon.

Neither should be read as supporting a shared/non-local consciousness substrate; both are recorded here
because CLAUDE.md requires logging the specific studies and replication status, not omitting them.

## Domain-level tensions spotted (for the synthesizer / vetter)

1. **COGITATE's actual result is more ambiguous than either "confirms IIT" or "confirms GNWT" headlines
   suggest**, and the two theory camps still dispute the interpretation of their own preregistered study
   post-hoc. Any synthesis citing COGITATE should state the specific confirmed/disconfirmed predictions, not
   a theory-level verdict.
2. **The AWARE-II "39% recalled awareness" statistic is doing a lot of rhetorical work relative to its
   evidentiary weight** — it comes from a 28-patient retrospective-interview subsample of a 567-patient
   study, and the study's own embedded objective test of perception during arrest (implicit auditory
   learning) returned a near-chance result. Popular coverage tends to foreground the recall statistic and
   drop the null objective-test result; the vetter should watch for this asymmetry propagating into synthesis.
3. **Wiest (2025) is a single-author review/argument paper, not new primary data** for most of its claims
   (it aggregates prior experiments); its most dramatic claim (macroscopic quantum entanglement in the living
   brain, via Kerskens & Pérez) is explicitly flagged by Wiest himself as contested. This is weaker sourcing
   than the anesthesia-pharmacology results it also cites, and the two should not be treated as equally solid
   within the same claim (I have kept them together in one claim per the brief's scope but flagged the
   asymmetry in the objection field).
4. **Illusionism (behind Attention Schema Theory) and non-local/filtration readings are direct rivals**,
   both at E4 (neither empirically decidable), representing the two philosophical poles the empirical
   material in this domain gets stretched toward. The synthesizer should treat this as an open tension, not
   resolve it.
5. **Predictive processing / free-energy principle underlies both mainstream cognitive neuroscience AND the
   psychedelics ALBUS model**, but critics have raised a live falsifiability concern (the "dark room
   problem," category-error critiques) about the framework itself — this bears on the falsifiability rule and
   is worth the vetter's attention if predictive processing gets used elsewhere in the corpus as a load-bearing
   mechanism.
6. **Psi's two flagship paradigms (Bem precognition, Ganzfeld telepathy) do not have equivalent evidentiary
   status** despite both being "parapsychology" — Bem has a clean disconfirming replication record (E6);
   Ganzfeld has an unresolved methodological dispute without a comparably clean disconfirmation (E3 heavy
   caveats). Lumping them together would over- or under-state the case in either direction.

## Source quality note

All 14 claims resolve to at least one live, peer-reviewed or otherwise credible source (Nature, Neuroscience
of Consciousness, Resuscitation, PNAS, Pharmacological Reviews, Psychological Bulletin, Alzheimer's &
Dementia, PLOS ONE, Frontiers, PMC/PubMed mirrors). No claim in this batch is UNVERIFIED; where a source's
own conclusions are contested (Wiest 2025's entanglement claim; the Storm/Hyman meta-analytic dispute), that
contestation is stated explicitly in the claim's `strongest_objection` field rather than smoothed over.
