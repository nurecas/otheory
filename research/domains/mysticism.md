# Mysticism — Domain Survey

**Phase:** SURVEY (breadth-first). **Researcher focus per brief:** contemplative phenomenology and its
scientific study, with a strict three-tier split between (a) first-person contemplative report, (b)
empirical measures of mystical/altered states, and (c) metaphysical conclusions drawn from them.

Note: `advaita-nonduality` (E5) was seeded prior to this survey (comparative-religion angle: Ātman=Brahman
as a tradition's own metaphysical claim) and is not duplicated here. This survey instead covers the
cross-tradition phenomenology of unitive/mystical experience, its scholarly typology and epistemology, and
the empirical program (psychometrics, psychedelic research, meditation neuroscience) that studies it.

## Coverage map

### 1. Phenomenology / contemplative report (E5)
- **`stace-common-core-typology`** — W.T. Stace's (1960) seven-feature typology of extrovertive and
  introvertive mystical experience (unity, non-spatiotemporality, noetic quality, positive affect,
  sacredness, paradoxicality, ineffability), presented as a descriptive taxonomy of first-person reports.
- **`forman-pure-consciousness-event`** — Robert Forman's (1990) "Pure Consciousness Event": reports of
  remaining awake/aware while devoid of all sensory or conceptual content, drawn from Buddhist, Christian,
  and modern contemplative testimony.
- **`jhana-samadhi-absorption-states`** — the Buddhist jhāna/samādhi sequence: a trained, graded,
  internally-consistent taxonomy of absorption states from the Pali canon and Abhidhamma/Visuddhimagga
  exegetical tradition.

### 2. The perennialism/constructivism dispute (E4 — scholarly epistemology, not report or data)
- **`perennialism-constructivism-debate`** — Steven Katz's (1978) "hard constructivism" (conceptual scheme
  wholly determines experiential content, so no common core exists) versus Stace/Forman-style essentialism.
  Explicitly filed as an unresolved *argument about how to read the reports*, not itself a report or an
  empirical finding.

### 3. Empirical measurement (E2/E3 — psychometrics, psychedelic research, neuroscience)
- **`meq30-mystical-experience-psychometrics`** — the 30-item Mystical Experience Questionnaire (Barrett,
  Johnson & Griffiths 2015): a validated, replicated four-factor self-report instrument built on Stace's
  categories, with demonstrated predictive validity for durable attitude/behavior change.
- **`hood-mysticism-scale-psychometrics`** — Ralph Hood's M-Scale (1975; three-factor cross-cultural
  confirmation 2001, US/Iran): operationalizes Stace's criteria and statistically separates raw
  phenomenology from religious interpretation.
- **`griffiths-psilocybin-mystical-experience`** — the Johns Hopkins double-blind psilocybin studies
  (Griffiths et al. 2006, 2008): high-dose psilocybin under supportive conditions reliably occasions
  "mystical-type" experiences (by Stace/Pahnke criteria) with durable, observer-corroborated positive
  effects at 14-month follow-up.
- **`psychedelic-ego-dissolution-dmn`** — Carhart-Harris et al. (2012, 2016): psilocybin/LSD reduce
  default-mode-network integrity, correlating with self-reported "ego dissolution."
- **`meditation-dmn-neuroscience`** — Brewer et al. (2011) and related work: experienced meditators show
  reduced DMN self-referential activity relative to novices, both during meditation and at rest.

### 4. Epistemology — does any of this count as evidence for its metaphysical content? (E4)
- **`mystical-experience-epistemic-status`** — the SEP-documented debate (perceptual model / Swinburne vs.
  doxastic-practice defense / Alston, against cross-checkability objections / Fales and the religious-
  diversity problem). No consensus that mystical experience is evidence for its apparent object; this claim
  is the load-bearing fence between "the experience is real and measurable" and "the experience's content
  is true."

## The three-tier split, stated plainly

1. **E5 — report.** That people across traditions and practices describe recurring, structured
   experiences (unity, ineffability, dissolution of self/world boundary, noetic certainty) is a
   phenomenological fact about human testimony. Not in serious dispute as *reportage*.
2. **E2/E3 — empirical measure.** That these reports can be operationalized into reliable, replicated
   psychometric instruments (MEQ30, M-Scale), that specific interventions (high-dose psilocybin, sustained
   meditation) reliably increase scores on those instruments and produce measurable downstream effects
   (well-being, prosociality, brain-network changes), is genuine empirical science, appropriately tiered
   E3 given open questions of blinding/expectancy, cross-cultural construct validity, and modest sample
   sizes. This is "evidence," properly hedged.
3. **E4 — metaphysical interpretation.** That any of this indicates a real transcendent unity, a
   mind-independent non-dual ground, or "contact" with something beyond the ordinary self is a live,
   unresolved philosophical question (per SEP), not an established conclusion. The reality and even the
   reliability of the *experience* (tiers 1-2) never entails the truth of its *content* (tier 3). This
   report never allows the first two tiers to be cited in support of the third.

## Domain-level tensions spotted

- **The core tension the whole domain runs on:** almost every popular and much scholarly writing on
  mysticism blurs "this experience is real/reliable/measurable" into "therefore its content is true" —
  exactly the laundering CLAUDE.md prohibits. The claims above are written to make that seam visible and
  keep it un-sealed.
- **Stace/Forman vs. Katz is unresolved and probably unresolvable by more data alone** — it turns on how
  "experience" and "concept" relate, a conceptual question, not an empirical one. Any downstream synthesis
  that treats "common core" as settled is overclaiming; so is treating constructivism as having refuted
  perennialism outright. Both are live positions.
- **Psychometric validation (MEQ30, M-Scale) partially operationalizes Stace's categories, which risks
  circularity**: instruments built on Stace's typology will tend to confirm a Stace-shaped structure in the
  data, which is weaker evidence for "a real cross-cultural common core" than it may look at first glance.
  Flagged explicitly in both psychometric claim files.
- **Functional unblinding in psychedelic trials** (participants/raters can usually tell active drug from
  active comparator) is a serious, insufficiently-resolved confound for the Griffiths studies' causal
  claims about "mystical-type experience -> lasting well-being," noted in that claim's objection.
  Self-selection is an analogous confound for the meditation-DMN literature (cross-sectional
  meditator-vs-novice designs).
- **Overlap/boundary with `advaita-nonduality` (seeded, E5):** that claim represents Advaita Vedānta's own
  metaphysical position (Ātman=Brahman) as the tradition's claim. The claims here are about the
  *phenomenology* (Stace, Forman, jhāna) and its *empirical study* (MEQ30, psilocybin, DMN), which are
  religiously and philosophically not identified with any one tradition's doctrine — deliberately kept
  distinct so a bridge-layer synthesis could later connect them (register: analogy/metaphor at most, not
  identity) without this domain file pre-judging that connection.
  Relevant future bridge candidates (for the synthesizer, not asserted here): O Theory's "widened valve"
  metaphor could connect `griffiths-psilocybin-mystical-experience` / `meditation-dmn-neuroscience`
  (E2/E3 mechanism candidates) to `advaita-nonduality` / `forman-pure-consciousness-event` (E5 reports) —
  but any such connection must be labeled speculation or metaphor, never shared-mathematics or evidence.
- **Psi/non-locality is out of scope here** — no claims in this file assert or investigate paranormal
  causation; the empirical claims are ordinary neuropharmacology and neuroimaging.

## Coverage gaps / candidates for DEEP-DIVE

- Christian apophatic/kataphatic mystical theology (e.g., Pseudo-Dionysius, John of the Cross, Teresa of
  Ávila) and Sufi fanā' — not covered in this pass; would enrich the perennialism debate's primary-source
  base beyond Stace's own citations.
- Near-death experiences (NDEs) as a distinct but overlapping altered-state literature (Greyson scale) —
  not covered; likely warrants its own claim(s) if the lead wants it folded into "mysticism" rather than
  filed under consciousness-science/anesthesia.
- The 2006/2008 Griffiths studies' precise dose-response and psychological-preparation/"set-and-setting"
  literature (Barrett & Griffiths reviews) — surveyed at the level of the two anchor papers; a deep-dive
  would read the full methods and the subsequent replication/extension literature (e.g., the 2011 dose-
  response follow-up, cancer-anxiety trials) in more depth.
- Jhāna-specific neuroimaging (as opposed to general meditation-DMN work) is thin; flagged as an open gap
  in `jhana-samadhi-absorption-states` rather than papered over.
- William James's *Varieties of Religious Experience* (1902) — the historical ancestor of Stace's four
  marks (ineffability, noetic quality, transiency, passivity) — not separately filed; folded by reference
  into `stace-common-core-typology`'s lineage but a dedicated claim could be added if the lead wants the
  historical precursor tracked separately from Stace's own typology.
