# Quantum Foundations — Domain Survey

Survey phase, breadth-first. Covers the measurement problem, the empirical anchors (Born rule, Bell's
theorem, Kochen-Specker contextuality), the major interpretations, the Wigner's-friend/Frauchiger-Renner
no-go result, and the von Neumann-Wigner consciousness-collapse interpretation. Two claims
(`qm-decoherence`, `many-worlds-interpretation`) were seeded before this survey and are not duplicated here;
this file treats them as part of the landscape and cross-references them.

## The central fact this domain has to get right

O Theory reads QM's "observer" and "nonlocality" as evidence for a nonlocal consciousness field. Mainstream
quantum foundations uses "observer" in at least three non-equivalent senses that must not be collapsed into
one:

1. **The physics observer (E1).** In decoherence theory, Copenhagen, relational QM, and consistent
   histories, an "observation" or "measurement" is any physical interaction that entangles a system with a
   sufficiently large/complex environment or apparatus — a photon hitting a detector, an electron
   scattering off another electron. No mind, awareness, or biological brain is required. This sense is
   established physics (`qm-decoherence`).
2. **The technical "context"/"agent" observer (E2).** Kochen-Specker "contextuality," QBism's "agent," and
   relational QM's "system relative to another system" are formal notions about which joint measurements are
   compatible, or about a rational belief-holder's epistemic state — again not about consciousness per se.
   Confusing this with sense 3 is one of the most common popularizations' errors (`kochen-specker-contextuality`,
   `qbism-quantum-bayesianism`, `relational-quantum-mechanics`).
3. **The literal consciousness-causes-collapse observer (E3).** Only von Neumann-Wigner, and its modern
   defenses (Stapp; Chalmers & McQueen 2022), claim that a conscious mind, specifically, does physical work
   in producing a definite outcome. This is a named, minority position within physics/philosophy of physics,
   explicitly not the mainstream reading of Copenhagen, and undercut by decoherence (which removes the
   original motivation for pushing the "cut" all the way to a mind) and by the practical near-unfalsifiability
   of most proposed tests (de Barros & Oas 2017). See `von-neumann-wigner-consciousness-collapse`.

O Theory's move from "QM has an observer effect" to "consciousness is fundamental" is licensed, if at all,
only by sense 3 — a contested E3 minority position — never by senses 1 or 2, which are mainstream and
mind-independent. The corpus keeps these three senses in physically and philosophically separate claim
files for exactly this reason.

## Tier distribution

- **E1 (established):** 4 — the measurement-problem statement, Born-rule empirics, Bell/nonlocality,
  Kochen-Specker contextuality. Plus seed `qm-decoherence`. These are the load-bearing facts; none of them,
  read correctly, mentions or requires consciousness.
- **E2 (mainstream-speculative):** 7 — Copenhagen, Bohmian mechanics, GRW/CSL, relational QM, consistent
  histories, the Frauchiger-Renner theorem's interpretive upshot, plus seed `many-worlds-interpretation`.
  All mathematically serious, none experimentally selected over its rivals.
- **E3 (heterodox/minority):** 1 — von Neumann-Wigner consciousness-collapse. Held by some credentialed
  physicists/philosophers (Wigner, Stapp; recent technical defense by Chalmers & McQueen 2022) but a
  minority view, not mainstream Copenhagen, and empirically hard to distinguish from decoherence-only
  accounts.
- **E4 (philosophical):** 1 — QBism, tiered here (not E2) because its core content is a stance on what
  probability/quantum states mean for any rational agent, not a competing physical mechanism.

No E5 or E6 claims were needed in this domain; nothing surveyed is symbolic/contemplative material or
failed pseudoscience once correctly tiered (note: von Neumann-Wigner is heterodox E3, not E6, because it is
a coherent, falsifiable-in-principle physical hypothesis defended in peer-reviewed venues, even though most
concrete tests of it are currently infeasible).

## Observation vs. interpretation — the key split

- **Observation (E1):** Bell inequalities are violated in loophole-free experiments; this rules out local
  hidden-variable theories. Kochen-Specker contextuality is observed. Decoherence timescales match
  prediction.
- **Interpretation (E2-E4):** What this MEANS for the nature of reality — many worlds actually exist, or
  particles have hidden trajectories, or collapse is a new physical process, or "nonlocality" reflects a
  deeper interconnectedness, or consciousness is doing causal work — is exactly what is NOT settled by the
  observations. Every interpretation on this list reproduces the identical Born-rule statistics; none is
  currently favored by experiment except GRW/CSL, which is distinguished only in the sense that it makes
  novel, so-far-unconfirmed predictions that could in principle rule it in or out.

## Where mainstream foundations stands on "observer = consciousness"

The overwhelming mainstream view (decoherence theorists, Bohmians, Everettians, QBists, relational-QM
proponents, consistent-historians) explicitly rejects any special physical role for consciousness in
collapse or measurement — measurement is "just" a physical interaction, of whatever complexity. Only von
Neumann-Wigner and its handful of modern defenders maintain the literal consciousness-collapse claim, and
even they generally frame it as a serious but unresolved research question rather than an established
result. Surveys and textbooks in quantum foundations (see Schlosshauer's decoherence literature, cited under
`qm-decoherence`) treat consciousness-collapse as a historical curiosity revisited by a small number of
specialists, not a live mainstream contender.

## Coverage gaps / candidates for deep-dive phase

- **Quantum Darwinism** (Zurek) — how einselection produces objective, redundantly-recorded classical
  information — not filed as a separate claim yet; currently folded conceptually into `qm-decoherence`.
- **Superdeterminism and retrocausal interpretations** (Hossenfelder, 't Hooft; two-state-vector formalism,
  Aharonov) — legitimate but small research programs that reject Bell's "measurement independence"
  assumption rather than locality; not covered as their own claims in this pass.
- **PBR theorem** (Pusey-Barrett-Rudolph, on whether the wavefunction is "real" / psi-ontic vs psi-epistemic)
  — directly relevant to QBism vs. realist interpretations; not yet filed.
- **Quantum Zeno effect and its role in testing Chalmers-McQueen-style consciousness-collapse models** —
  mentioned in the von-neumann-wigner claim's sources but not separately filed.
- **Delayed-choice and quantum-eraser experiments** — frequently misused in popular writing to suggest
  retrocausal or observer-dependent reality; worth a dedicated claim to pre-empt misreadings, not filed yet.
- Deep-dive phase should have the vetter confirm whether `wigners-friend-frauchiger-renner`'s tier (E2) is
  right versus splitting the proven no-go theorem (which would be E1, like `kochen-specker-contextuality`)
  from its contested interpretive morals (E2) — flagged here as a domain-level tension.

## Domain-level tensions spotted

1. **Tension within the seed set:** `qm-decoherence` is E1 as a mechanism but explicitly leaves the
   measurement problem open; `measurement-problem-statement` (this pass) makes that gap precise. These two
   claims should be read together, not as competing.
2. **Tension in tiering logical theorems vs. their morals:** Kochen-Specker's theorem is filed as E1 (both
   proof and confirmed prediction), while Frauchiger-Renner's theorem is filed as E2 — the proof itself is as
   valid as any mathematics, but its *significance* (which premise to reject) is what's contested. The
   vetter should decide whether this asymmetry is principled (Kochen-Specker's empirical content is directly
   tested; Frauchiger-Renner's is a thought experiment not yet performed with real agents) or whether
   Frauchiger-Renner's core theorem should also be split into an E1 (valid derivation) + E2 (interpretive
   upshot) pair.
3. **Tension for the synthesizer, not resolved here:** almost every interpretation surveyed (Copenhagen,
   Bohmian, GRW/CSL, QBism, relational QM, consistent histories, many-worlds) explicitly removes or avoids
   any special role for consciousness — only von Neumann-Wigner keeps it, and it is a minority view under
   pressure from decoherence and near-unfalsifiability. Any cross-domain bridge from "QM" to "consciousness
   as substrate" should be honestly registered as resting on the E3 minority claim alone, capped there, and
   never inherit the E1 confidence of Bell's theorem or decoherence.
