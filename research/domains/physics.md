# Physics — domain survey (SURVEY phase)

## Scope of this pass

Breadth-first inventory of the physics claims most relevant to O Theory's reach toward "the quantum vacuum
potential or fundamental spacetime geometry" as a substrate. Eleven new claim files were filed in this pass
(listed below), in addition to the three physics-relevant seed claims already in the index
(`noether-symmetry-conservation`, `qm-decoherence`, `many-worlds-interpretation`, plus `e8-lie-group` /
`lisi-e8-toe` which sit jointly in physics and mathematics-geometry). Together these give reasonable
coverage of: established 20th-century physics (GR, the Standard Model, thermodynamics), the current
quantum-gravity landscape (string/M-theory, loop quantum gravity, asymptotic safety, causal sets), and the
holographic/information-theoretic cluster (AdS/CFT, black-hole entropy, Verlinde's emergent gravity, and the
technical physics behind Wheeler's it-from-bit), matching the brief's required coverage list.

Note: other domain-researcher passes running in parallel (metaphysics, consciousness-science,
quantum-foundations, mathematics-geometry, esoteric, mysticism) have independently filed adjacent claims
under `research/claims/`, including a metaphysics-domain claim for Wheeler's "it from bit" itself
(`it-from-bit-information-ontology`, E4) and a related `pancomputationalism-digital-physics` (E4). To avoid
duplicating those, the physics-domain claim filed here
(`wheeler-it-from-bit-information-physics`) was scoped narrowly to the citable, peer-reviewed *technical*
physics the slogan inspired (the Chiribella-D'Ariano-Perinotti informational axiomatization of quantum
theory, E2) rather than restating the ontological thesis, which is cross-referenced instead.

## Landscape

**Tier E1 — established, textbook physics** (`general-relativity-spacetime-curvature`,
`standard-model-gauge-theory`, `second-law-thermodynamics-entropy`, `black-hole-entropy-area-law`, plus seed
claims `noether-symmetry-conservation` and `qm-decoherence`). This is the bedrock: repeatedly, precisely
confirmed, mainstream-consensus physics. Even here there are open edges worth flagging for a deep-dive: GR
is not a quantum theory and requires dark matter/dark energy to fit cosmological data; the Standard Model
has ~19 unexplained free parameters and no gravity or dark-matter sector; the second law's ultimate
justification (why the universe began low-entropy) is unresolved; and the Bekenstein-Hawking area law,
while rigorously derived, directly produces the unresolved black-hole information paradox. None of these
open edges licenses any claim beyond E1 for the core established results themselves — but they are exactly
where popularizations often overreach into speculation dressed as fact.

**Tier E2 — mainstream-speculative, mathematically developed but unconfirmed** (`string-m-theory-unification`,
`loop-quantum-gravity`, `asymptotic-safety-quantum-gravity`, `causal-set-theory`,
`holographic-principle-ads-cft`, `wheeler-it-from-bit-information-physics`, plus seed claim
`many-worlds-interpretation`). This is the most important finding of the survey: there are at least **four
mutually exclusive, unconfirmed** research programs competing to be the theory of quantum gravity (strings,
loops, asymptotic safety, causal sets), each mathematically serious and pursued by credentialed physicists,
none with a single confirmed experimental prediction distinguishing it from the others or from "no new
physics." AdS/CFT (Maldacena 1997) is the best internally-tested mathematical duality in the group —
extraordinarily productive as a calculational tool — but it is a conjecture about idealized anti-de Sitter
spacetimes, not a proven statement about our own (de Sitter, positive-Λ) universe. This tier is where
O Theory's "hidden dimensions," "vibrating strings," and "holographic reality" language lives, and none of
it should be presented as confirmed physics.

**Tier E3 — heterodox/minority, published but contested** (`verlinde-entropic-emergent-gravity`, plus seed
claim `lisi-e8-toe`). Verlinde's proposal that gravity/spacetime are wholly emergent from entanglement
entropy, potentially eliminating dark matter, is peer-reviewed (JHEP, SciPost) and intellectually serious,
but faces a strong empirical challenge (the Bullet Cluster) and an unresolved technical dispute (Dai &
Stojkovic 2017 vs. Yoon 2020) about internal consistency. It sits closest, among all physics claims
surveyed, to O Theory's own "gravity/reality as emergent from information" framing — which makes it
important to be precise that even Verlinde does not extend the proposal to consciousness, and that the
mainstream community largely still favors particle dark matter.

**Tier E4 — philosophical, not empirically decidable (filed by the metaphysics domain pass, cross-referenced
here).** Wheeler's "it from bit" *as an ontological thesis* — that reality "is" information at bottom —
is filed under `it-from-bit-information-ontology` (metaphysics domain), not duplicated here. The physics
claim in this domain (`wheeler-it-from-bit-information-physics`) deliberately covers only the separable,
citable technical result (informational reconstruction of quantum theory's formalism) and states plainly
that it does not establish the stronger ontological claim. This split is itself the main finding relevant to
O Theory's information-as-substrate language: a real E2 technical result exists, and a separate E4
philosophical thesis rides on top of it — they must not be cited interchangeably.

## Domain-level tensions spotted

1. **Quantum-gravity pluralism with no adjudicator.** String/M-theory, loop quantum gravity, asymptotic
   safety, and causal set theory are mutually incompatible research programs (different, sometimes
   contradictory, assumptions about whether spacetime is discrete, whether extra dimensions exist, whether
   supersymmetry is required) that have coexisted for 30-50 years without any experiment favoring one over
   the others. A deep-dive should resist treating any one of them as "the" quantum-gravity answer.

2. **The swampland/landscape crisis inside string theory itself.** Even within the leading candidate,
   there is now a live, published dispute (Palti 2019 review; Woit's commentary on Strings 2024) about
   whether the "swampland" research program yields sharp, falsifiable constraints or has become an
   unfalsifiable exercise in classification. This is worth flagging prominently in any synthesis that
   leans on string theory for anything beyond "an actively studied candidate."

3. **Information-theoretic physics is genuinely two different claims routinely merged.** (a) Information-
   theoretic quantities (entropy, entanglement, bits) doing real mathematical work in established and
   speculative physics (black-hole entropy, AdS/CFT, informational axiomatizations of QM) — this is solid,
   citable technical content, E1/E2. (b) The metaphysical claim that reality "is" information "at bottom,"
   per Wheeler's slogan — this is philosophy (filed as E4 under the metaphysics domain), not physics,
   however much (a) motivates it. O Theory's language risks collapsing these two; the claim files in this
   domain (especially `wheeler-it-from-bit-information-physics`) are written specifically to keep them
   apart and cross-reference rather than duplicate the metaphysics-domain treatment.

4. **Verlinde vs. the Bullet Cluster is unresolved and should not be minimized either way.** The strongest
   E3 candidate in this survey (emergent gravity) faces what many physicists consider close to a knockout
   empirical objection (Bullet Cluster lensing), yet the debate about the internal consistency of the
   theory (Dai-Stojkovic vs. Yoon) is still open in the literature as of 2020. Neither "emergent gravity
   is basically dead" nor "emergent gravity is a live alternative to dark matter" is an honest one-line
   summary; both need the caveat stated.

5. **Popular-science conflation is the main external risk, not internal physics dispute.** AdS/CFT
   ("holographic universe"), quantum vacuum fluctuations, and "it from bit" are the three physics
   ideas most frequently stripped of their actual (narrow, technical, unconfirmed-for-our-universe)
   content and re-packaged as support for consciousness-related metaphysics. The claim files above
   are written specifically to make that gap visible and citable.

## Coverage gaps for a future deep-dive

- **Cosmic inflation** (seed-listed as E2-adjacent in CLAUDE.md's own tier examples) was not filed as a
  separate claim in this pass; it deserves one, given its centrality to cosmology and its own
  falsifiability debates (multiverse implications, non-uniqueness of predictions).
- **Quantum field theory in curved spacetime / semiclassical gravity** beyond black-hole thermodynamics
  (e.g. Unruh effect, cosmological particle production) was only touched via Hawking radiation; a fuller
  treatment would strengthen the E1/E2 boundary discussion.
- **Supersymmetry** was mentioned only as an unconfirmed prediction of string theory; it could be filed as
  its own claim given its independent history and the LHC's null results to date.
- **Quantum vacuum energy / zero-point energy and the cosmological-constant problem** (the ~120-order-of-
  magnitude discrepancy between QFT vacuum-energy predictions and the observed cosmological constant) is a
  major, well-known E1-adjacent puzzle directly relevant to O Theory's "quantum vacuum as O-Source"
  language and was not filed as a standalone claim in this pass — a priority for the deep-dive.
- **Analog gravity experiments** (Bose-Einstein-condensate "sonic black holes" measuring Hawking-like
  radiation) were mentioned only inside the black-hole-entropy claim's falsifiability note; they deserve
  their own claim given they are actual laboratory evidence bearing on quantum-gravity-adjacent physics.
- **Penrose's cyclic conformal cosmology / Penrose-Hameroff beyond Orch-OR** (already partly covered by the
  seed claim `orch-or-microtubules` in consciousness-science/quantum-foundations) was not re-examined here
  from the pure-cosmology side.
- Deeper primary-source reconstruction of the Distler-Garibaldi no-go proof (already seeded for `lisi-e8-toe`)
  and of the GW250114 black-hole-spectroscopy methodology would belong to a DEEP-DIVE pass, not this survey.
