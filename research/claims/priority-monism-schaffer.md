steelman: "Schaffer argues priority monism is favored by a 'Laser' (a restricted Occam's razor: do not multiply fundamental/basic entities beyond necessity, though many derivative entities may still exist), by the physical fact that the universe's parts are demonstrably entangled and non-separable in quantum mechanics (his 'argument from quantum emergence': the entangled system's state does not supervene on the intrinsic properties of its parts, suggesting the whole is not a mere sum built from independent parts), and by the coherence of holding existence pluralism (many things exist: persons, particles, furniture) together with priority monism (only the cosmic whole is metaphysically basic; everything else exists only as a dependent fragment of it)."
strongest_objection: "Priority monism faces the challenge of explaining how a single, simple fundamental whole can ground the evident heterogeneity and structure of the world's parts without covertly reintroducing those parts as independently basic; critics also press that quantum entanglement, while showing non-separability of some systems, is a specific, limited physical phenomenon and does not obviously license a fully general metaphysical claim that literally everything (including systems with no measured entanglement) is a dependent fragment of one cosmic whole."
falsifiability: "Not empirically decidable — a metaphysical thesis about relations of ontological priority/dependence, argued by philosophical reasoning (parsimony, coherence with physics) rather than settled by any possible experiment."

Priority monism holds that exactly one concrete entity is fundamental — the whole cosmos — and that all its parts (particles, people, planets) are real but merely dependent, derivative aspects of that single basic whole, reversing the more common assumption that wholes are built up from independently-existing parts. This is a structurally important precedent for any "ground of being" framing (including O Theory's O-Source as a single underlying substrate from which apparent multiplicity derives), and is closely related to but logically distinct from cosmopsychism (already filed): priority monism is a general thesis about parts/whole dependence and need not attribute mentality to the cosmic whole, whereas cosmopsychism specifically holds the whole is a conscious subject. Tier E4: argued via parsimony and interpretation of quantum non-separability, not a testable physical claim.
