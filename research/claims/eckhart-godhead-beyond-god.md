Meister Eckhart distinguishes the revealed, Trinitarian "God" from a prior "Godhead" (Gottheit) — simple, nameless, "without mode" — and teaches that the innermost ground of the soul, stripped of all created images through detachment (Gelassenheit), is one with this Godhead. Represented here as Eckhart's own mystical-theological claim (E5), historically controversial enough that some formulations were condemned by Rome in 1329; any O-Theory analogy between "Godhead" and an "O-Source" is metaphor across traditions, not evidence, and elides real doctrinal risk this claim carried within its own tradition.
