Time-translation symmetry gives energy conservation; spatial-translation gives momentum; rotational gives angular momentum. This is a genuine mathematical theorem with a proof, not an empirical hypothesis, and it underwrites the deep link between symmetry and physical law that runs through all of modern physics."
