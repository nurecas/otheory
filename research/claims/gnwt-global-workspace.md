steelman: "GNWT (Dehaene, Changeux, Baars) proposes conscious access occurs when a sensory representation is amplified by attention and 'ignites' a broadly distributed fronto-parietal network, making the information globally available to memory, language, and action systems. It has strong support from decades of masking/attentional-blink experiments showing a nonlinear 'ignition' threshold and has motivated clinically useful markers (e.g., P3b event-related potentials) that track reportable perception across many paradigms."
strongest_objection: "The 2025 COGITATE adversarial test found some confirming signals (transient ignition in occipito-temporal and frontal regions, content decoding in a 200-800ms window) but did NOT find GNWT's predicted sharp 'offset' ignition when conscious perception ended, and content decoding in prefrontal cortex was weaker than predicted — while posterior cortex alone proved sufficient for sustained content representation. GNWT proponents (e.g., Naccache et al.) dispute that offset ignition was ever a central prediction and argue prefrontal decoding failures reflect methodological limits (mixed selectivity), not theory failure. The theory has also been criticized for conflating consciousness with the cognitive/executive functions (reportability, working memory) that piggyback on it, a criticism raised independently of COGITATE. Given the contested post-hoc dispute over what COGITATE actually falsified, GNWT remains a seriously developed but empirically unresolved theory — E3, not established."
falsifiability: "Yes — GNWT makes specific anatomical (fronto-parietal involvement) and temporal (ignition onset/offset dynamics) predictions that were directly tested by COGITATE and partly disconfirmed, with an active post-publication dispute over interpretation. This is a genuinely falsifiable research program, currently in a state of contested partial disconfirmation rather than confirmation."

Interpretation/theory-level claim, distinct from the raw NCC observations (see ncc-research-program) and from the COGITATE study's findings themselves (see cogitate-adversarial-collaboration-2025). GNWT is one of the two theories COGITATE was designed to adjudicate between; it survived some tests and failed others, and its proponents dispute the failure's significance — the honest state is unresolved, contested E3, not vindication for either side.

Red-team note (2026-07-07): sources verified (Mashour et al. 2020 Neuron review, and the 2025 COGITATE Nature paper). Tier E3 and status "disputed" are correct and now made explicit in the status field (was "draft"): this is precisely the contested-minority-view case CLAUDE.md describes — a credentialed research program with real predictions, partially confirmed and partially disconfirmed by a rigorous adversarial test, with an active, unresolved post-publication dispute (Naccache et al. vs. the consortium's reported reading) about what the disconfirmation means. Must not be stated as "confirmed by COGITATE" in any synthesis document — it is one of two theories that survived some and failed other of its own preregistered predictions.
