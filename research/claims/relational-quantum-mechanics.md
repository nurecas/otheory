steelman: "Generalizing the role of Bohr's 'observer' to any physical system whatsoever (not just macroscopic/classical apparatus, and certainly not requiring consciousness), RQM holds that a quantum system has properties only relative to another system it has interacted with; there is no view from nowhere, only a network of relative facts. This removes the privileged classical/quantum or observer/observed divide of Copenhagen and gives a uniform account of measurement as ordinary physical interaction between any two systems, at any scale."
strongest_objection: "Facing the same challenge as QBism, RQM must explain why relative facts for different 'observers' (any two interacting systems) cohere into the single shared, intersubjective world we experience — a tension sharpened by the Frauchiger-Renner theorem, which some argue poses a direct problem for RQM's claim that all perspectives can be consistently reconciled. It is also, like other interpretations, empirically equivalent to standard quantum mechanics, so it is adopted for its conceptual economy, not because of any distinguishing evidence."
falsifiability: "Empirically indistinguishable from other no-collapse/relational readings of the formalism; RQM's claims are about the semantics of 'fact' and 'state,' not new dynamics, so there is no possible experiment that would confirm or refute it as against, e.g., QBism or many-worlds."

RED-TEAM NOTES (2026-07-07): Both sources verified. Laudisa & Rovelli's SEP entry confirmed via WebFetch (current,
substantively revised Feb 2025) and directly quotes-confirms the core claims here: "the value a variable takes is
only relative to the (other) system affected by the interaction," and explicitly, "Subjects, or agents play no
special role in RQM... There is nothing subjective, idealistic, or mentalistic, in RQM." This is an unusually
clean, direct confirmation from the primary secondary source that the claim's own framing (RQM's "observer" is any
physical system, explicitly not consciousness) is accurate, not an interpretive stretch by the claim's author.
Rovelli (1996), International Journal of Theoretical Physics 35, 1637-1678 (arXiv:quant-ph/9609002), is the
correct original paper.

Tier confirmed E2, per CLAUDE.md's explicit list (relational QM named as an example E2 interpretation with no
distinguishing experiment). The claim's own designation of "mainstream-speculative... rather than pure philosophy"
is defensible: Rovelli is a working physicist and the framework connects to information-theoretic and
quantum-gravity motivations, though its core content remains interpretive and empirically equivalent to orthodox
QM — correctly reflected in the falsifiability field ("no possible experiment... as against, e.g., QBism or
many-worlds").

The closing note is well-supported and important: RQM is, if anything, the interpretation MOST hostile to
consciousness-collapse narratives, since it makes every physical interaction (electron-photon, thermometer-gas,
etc.) an "observation," diluting rather than elevating any special mental role. No changes needed beyond
confirming status.
