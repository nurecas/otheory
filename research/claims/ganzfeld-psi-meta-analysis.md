steelman: "Ganzfeld studies use a standardized sensory-deprivation free-response protocol specifically designed to reduce sensory leakage and experimenter effects; Storm, Tressoldi & Di Risio's (2010) meta-analysis of a homogeneous set of 29 studies (1997-2008) reported a mean effect size of 0.142 (Stouffer Z=5.48, p=2.13×10⁻⁸), and proponents argue this represents a small but statistically robust anomaly surviving decades of methodological refinement in response to earlier critiques (e.g., the original Hyman-Honorton 1986 debate)."
strongest_objection: "Ray Hyman's peer-reviewed comment on the same 2010 meta-analysis argued the authors 'manufacture apparent homogeneity and consistency by eliminating many outliers and combining databases whose combined effect sizes are not significantly different — even though these combined effect sizes consist of arbitrary and meaningless composites,' and that at best their study 'provides a recipe for conducting a replicable extrasensory perception experiment' using selected subjects and standard protocol rather than demonstrating a robust, subject-independent anomaly — i.e., positive results cluster in a small number of labs/experimenters (a classic experimenter-effect red flag), which is inconsistent with a robust, mechanism-independent physical phenomenon and consistent with uncontrolled variables, selective publication, or so-far-undetected protocol leakage concentrated in specific labs. No independent, adversarially preregistered, multi-lab replication with results audited by skeptic and proponent alike (comparable to COGITATE's design for consciousness theories) has been conducted for Ganzfeld psi. No mechanism consistent with known physics for the purported information transfer has been proposed or evidenced. Given a genuine, decades-long, credentialed-researcher literature that nonetheless shows a lab-dependent effect, unresolved meta-analytic disputes about study inclusion (Storm et al.'s own 2010 reply to Hyman does not resolve the homogeneity dispute, it restates their position), and no successful theory-neutral adversarial replication, this sits at E3-with-heavy-caveats rather than either established science or flatly rejected pseudoscience."
falsifiability: "Yes in principle — a large, preregistered, multi-site, theory-neutral (skeptic/proponent co-designed) replication, analogous to COGITATE, would be the decisive test and has not yet been run for Ganzfeld psi at that standard of rigor. Existing meta-analyses remain a contested statistical/methodological dispute rather than a resolved question."
