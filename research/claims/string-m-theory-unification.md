steelman: "String theory replaces point particles with one-dimensional extended objects (strings) whose vibrational modes correspond to different particle types, and it naturally includes a massless spin-2 excitation identified with the graviton — the only known framework where gravity emerges automatically from a consistent quantum theory. It is mathematically extremely rich (dualities, mirror symmetry, AdS/CFT emerged from it) and has driven major advances in pure mathematics and quantum field theory even independent of its status as physics. M-theory is the conjectured 11-dimensional theory unifying the five consistent 10-dimensional superstring theories."
strongest_objection: "After five decades of development, string/M-theory makes no confirmed, uniquely testable prediction at currently accessible energies: it requires extra spatial dimensions (compactified at unobserved scales), a vast 'landscape' of ~10^500 possible vacua with no known selection principle, and low-energy supersymmetry, which the LHC has not found at expected scales. The 'swampland' program (Palti 2019 review; constraining which effective field theories can arise from a consistent quantum-gravity UV completion) is itself contested — critics (e.g., Woit, commenting on Strings 2024) argue the research program risks becoming unfalsifiable in practice. No experiment has confirmed strings, extra dimensions, or supersymmetry."
falsifiability: "In principle falsifiable by direct detection of supersymmetric partners, extra dimensions (e.g., deviations from Newtonian gravity at sub-millimeter scales, or missing energy signatures at colliders), or specific stringy signatures in cosmological data (e.g., cosmic string signatures, non-Gaussianities). None of these has been detected as of 2026; the relevant energy scale (Planck scale, ~10^19 GeV) is far beyond any conceivable collider, making direct tests currently infeasible, though indirect cosmological/astrophysical tests remain an active area."

String/M-theory is the paradigmatic E2 claim: mathematically serious, actively developed by thousands of researchers, and taken seriously as a UV-complete candidate for quantum gravity, but with zero direct experimental confirmation after 50 years, and facing an active internal crisis (the swampland/landscape problem) about whether it can make sharp predictions at all. It should never be presented as an established unification; where O Theory or its sources invoke "hidden dimensions" or "vibrating strings" as physics for a non-local consciousness substrate, that borrows string theory's vocabulary without any of its actual (contested, unconfirmed) content — a speculation/metaphor register at best.
