steelman: "As a study, COGITATE is exceptionally rigorous: theory proponents and a neutral consortium jointly preregistered the experimental design, the divergent predictions, and how results would be interpreted before data collection — a rare adversarial-collaboration structure meant to prevent post-hoc rationalization. It found sustained representation of stimulus content in posterior cortex sufficient for conscious perception (as IIT predicted) but did not find IIT's predicted sustained gamma-band synchronization in that posterior 'hot zone'; it found transient prefrontal/fronto-parietal 'ignition' activity (as GNWT predicted) but not the predicted sharp offset-ignition when perception ended, and prefrontal content-decoding was weaker than GNWT anticipated. Neither theory was cleanly confirmed or refuted."
strongest_objection: "Despite the preregistration, the interpretation of what counts as 'confirmed' vs. 'disconfirmed' remains disputed post-publication: GNWT proponents (Naccache et al.) argue the paper mischaracterized which predictions were central to the theory and that some 'failures' reflect known methodological limits of decoding in prefrontal cortex (mixed selectivity) rather than genuine disconfirmation; IIT proponents (Boly, Koch, Tononi, per the IIT Wiki adversarial-collaboration supplement) similarly reconcile the gamma-synchrony null with technical limitations (sparse electrode coverage, ROI selection) while maintaining IIT's core principles are unaffected. This means the single-study result, however well-designed, has not yet produced consensus about which theory (if either) it favors — it is strong evidence about the specific tested predictions, not a settled verdict on IIT or GNWT as wholes."
falsifiability: "This is itself the falsification test: each theory staked out precise, preregistered, divergent predictions, and the experiment was run and reported. The tier is E1 for the empirical result (what was measured, under preregistration, replicated across three neuroimaging modalities) — it is separated here from the still-contested question of what the result means for IIT or GNWT as theories, which are filed at E3 in their own claims."

This claim isolates the STUDY as an empirical event — a well-controlled, preregistered, multi-modal experiment with a specific, reported data pattern — from the theory-level question of whether IIT or GNWT is thereby vindicated or refuted (see iit-integrated-information and gnwt-global-workspace, filed separately at E3). O Theory's essay narrative sometimes reads COGITATE as "confirming" one framework; the honest reading is that it confirmed a piece of each and refuted a piece of each, with the significance of the refutations still argued between the original theory teams.

Red-team note (2026-07-07): all three sources verified, including the IIT Wiki's own post-hoc supplement, which independently confirms the "disputed reconciliation" framing in strongest_objection is not one-sided — both GNWT and IIT camps engage in post-hoc reconciliation of their own null results, which is worth noting explicitly (added above) rather than singling out only GNWT proponents as disputing the result. E1 is appropriate for the study's execution and raw reported findings (this is real, replicated-across-modalities, preregistered data); E1 does NOT extend to "COGITATE settled the theory debate," which correctly remains fenced off to the E3 theory-level claims. This is a good example of the observation/interpretation split done right: the study is E1, both theories it tested remain E3.
