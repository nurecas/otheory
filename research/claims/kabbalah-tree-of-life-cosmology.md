steelman: "The ten sefirot (Keter, Chokhmah, Binah, Chesed, Gevurah, Tiferet, Netzach, Hod, Yesod, Malkuth) and the paths connecting them form a sophisticated, textually deep theological-cosmological system developed from the Sefer Yetzirah (early centuries CE) through the Sefer Bahir (12th c. Provence) to the Zohar (late 13th c. Spain, per Gershom Scholem's philological dating to Moses de Leon, c. 1280-1286), describing how the infinite (Ein Sof) emanates into structured, nameable divine attributes and ultimately the created world. It is a major, rigorously studied current within Jewish mysticism, not a fringe invention."
strongest_objection: "As theology and cosmology it is not empirically decidable — it is a symbolic and doctrinal structure whose authority rests on textual, mystical, and communal-religious tradition, not third-person verification. Symbol/practice, not fact."

The Tree of Life is E5: a genuine, historically deep esoteric cosmology within Jewish mysticism, represented on its own textual and scholarly terms (per Scholem). Relevant to O Theory as one of several "layered emanation" cosmological maps (alongside e.g. Advaita's Brahman/Ātman, already filed) that can be cited as a structural analogy or metaphor for tiered ontology — never as evidence for a physical hierarchy of reality.
