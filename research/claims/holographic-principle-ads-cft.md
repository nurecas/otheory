steelman: "'t Hooft and Susskind proposed, motivated by the Bekenstein-Hawking result that black-hole entropy scales with horizon area rather than volume, that a full description of any region's physics (including gravity) can be encoded on its lower-dimensional boundary. Maldacena's AdS/CFT correspondence is a concrete, mathematically precise conjectured duality (over 20,000 citations, unrefuted after nearly three decades of tests) equating type IIB string theory on AdS5xS5 with N=4 super Yang-Mills, a well-defined quantum field theory without gravity, on the boundary. It has become an extraordinarily productive calculational tool, exporting techniques between quantum gravity, condensed matter theory, and nuclear physics (e.g., quark-gluon plasma viscosity estimates)."
strongest_objection: "AdS/CFT is a conjecture, not a proven theorem: it has passed many highly non-trivial internal consistency checks (matching spectra, correlation functions, and entropy calculations between the two sides) but lacks a full proof, and it is formulated for anti-de Sitter space, which has a negative cosmological constant — the opposite sign from our own accelerating, positive-cosmological-constant (de Sitter) universe. Extending holography rigorously to realistic de Sitter/FRW cosmology remains unsolved, meaning it is not directly a description of our own universe's boundary physics without further (unconfirmed) conjectural extension."
falsifiability: "The core AdS/CFT duality itself is a mathematical conjecture about a specific (non-realistic, negative-cosmological-constant) class of spacetimes; its support so far consists of internal mathematical consistency checks (matching correlators, spectra, entropies across the duality) rather than laboratory experiment, so in the strict sense it is not independently falsifiable by observation the way a physical prediction is — its 'testing' is mathematical self-consistency. The FURTHER claim that the holographic principle describes our actual (de Sitter) universe is a separate, weaker, currently untested extension: no known experiment distinguishes a holographic from a non-holographic description of our universe at present, so that extension is not currently falsifiable and should not be stated with the same confidence as the AdS/CFT result itself."

The holographic principle and AdS/CFT are E2: a rigorously developed, highly cited, internally well-tested mathematical duality within string theory, not a confirmed statement about our own (de Sitter, positive-cosmological-constant) universe. This is the single most misused physics term in popular "consciousness is holographic" writing: AdS/CFT is a precise duality between two specific mathematical theories in an idealized spacetime, and invoking "reality is a hologram" for a non-local consciousness substrate is, at best, a loose analogy to the boundary/bulk structure — not a claim the physics licenses.
