steelman: "Panpsychism is motivated by Galen Strawson's anti-emergence argument: since experience cannot intelligibly arise from the wholly non-experiential (radical emergence being unintelligible, on this view), and since consciousness is undeniably real, some form of proto-mentality must already be present in the fundamental constituents of physical reality. Constitutive panpsychism additionally promises parsimony — one kind of stuff, not two — versus dualism, and locates mentality inside physics's causal structure rather than alongside it."
strongest_objection: "The combination problem, rooted in William James's objection to 'mind-dust' (micro-subjects being 'shut in its own skin, windowless'), holds that it is unintelligible how numerically distinct micro-subjects of experience could combine into one unified macro-subject without either begging the question or reintroducing an explanatory gap exactly like the one panpsychism was meant to close. SEP's panpsychism entry (sec. 4) confirms there is no widely accepted solution; it names the 'subject-summing' problem (sec. 4.3) as the paradigmatic form, plus the 'palette' problem (sec. 4.4.1, how a limited set of micro-qualities could ground the full richness of human experience) and the 'structural mismatch'/grain problem (sec. 4.4.2, continuous experience versus discrete brain structure)."
falsifiability: "Not empirically decidable even in principle — there is no possible measurement that would detect proto-experiential properties in electrons or settle whether micro-experiences combine. A metaphysical argument assessed by conceptual coherence and explanatory power; E4."

This claim isolates panpsychism proper (mentality distributed at the micro-level, bottom-up) and its central named objection, distinct from the already-seeded cosmopsychism (mentality at the cosmic level, top-down, facing the mirror-image "decombination problem"). O Theory's architecture is structurally closer to cosmopsychism/idealism than to bottom-up panpsychism, but panpsychism is the historically prior and more thoroughly debated position from which cosmopsychism was developed specifically to escape the combination problem — a move whose success is itself contested. Both directions remain E4 arguments, never findings.
