This claim is a historical correction, filed E5 because it concerns the honest classification of a symbolic/practice text, not a physics claim. The Kybalion (1908, "Three Initiates," widely attributed to William Walker Atkinson) is New Thought self-help dressed in invented antiquity, not a genuine ancient Hermetic transmission — the real primary source for Hermeticism is the Corpus Hermeticum (see `corpus-hermeticum-primary-source`). This distinction matters for O Theory: any "Hermetic" framing borrowed for correspondence/vibration imagery should cite the Corpus Hermeticum or scholarly sources, not the Kybalion's pseudo-ancient "Seven Principles," and should say so.
