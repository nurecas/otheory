The chakra/nadi system as a contemplative and symbolic map within yogic and tantric traditions is E5: a genuine, historically documented practice framework, honestly represented as that tradition's own map of subtle experience. This is the register O Theory should use if it invokes chakra/subtle-body imagery. The companion claim `chakra-energy-measurable-claim` handles the distinct, empirically-framed claim that chakras are physically detectable energy centers, which fails vetting.

**Red-team notes (2026-07-07) — source correction:** The draft cited Moga, M. (2022), "Is there scientific evidence for chakras?", International Journal of Healing and Caring, as reviewing "the traditional textual/practice framework alongside attempted empirical correlates." Having read the actual PDF: this is a mischaracterization on two counts. (1) IJHC is not an independent scientific journal — it is the house publication of the National Alliance of Energy Healing/National Alliance of Energy Practitioners, an energy-healing practitioner-advocacy organization, and its own author bio and journal masthead confirm this. (2) The article does not present a balanced or skeptical review of "attempted" correlates; its own conclusion affirmatively states that "radiations are detectable at chakra sites" and that chakras "may be scientifically delineated" — i.e., it argues FOR the empirical/measurable-energy claim, which belongs entirely to the companion E6 claim, not to this contemplative-practice claim. It has been removed from this file's sources as a poor fit (a practice-and-symbol claim does not need, and should not lean on, a contested empirical-measurement paper) and is discussed instead, correctly framed, under `chakra-energy-measurable-claim`. The remaining Wikipedia source was verified directly and accurately supports the textual/tantric origin claim. Tier E5 confirmed for this claim as narrowly scoped to practice/symbol; status set to vetted.
