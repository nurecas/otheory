steelman: "Loop quantum gravity (LQG) applies canonical quantization directly to general relativity using Ashtekar's reformulation of GR in connection variables, without assuming a fixed background spacetime. Its central mathematical result, proven by Rovelli and Smolin, is that the operators representing area and volume have discrete eigenvalue spectra — meaning space itself is quantized into finite chunks at the Planck scale, with quantum states of geometry represented by spin networks. This is a rigorous, non-perturbative, background-independent quantization, a genuine mathematical achievement distinct from string theory's approach."
strongest_objection: "LQG has not been shown to reproduce classical general relativity in the low-energy limit in a fully general way (the semiclassical limit problem remains open), struggles to naturally incorporate matter fields and the Standard Model, and has made no confirmed experimental prediction — proposed tests (e.g., Lorentz-invariance violation signatures in gamma-ray bursts, modified dispersion relations) have so far found null results consistent with ordinary GR, constraining rather than confirming the theory. It remains, like string theory, an unconfirmed quantum-gravity candidate."
falsifiability: "Falsifiable in principle via Planck-scale signatures: modified dispersion relations for high-energy photons (tested via gamma-ray burst timing, e.g. Fermi telescope data), predicted discreteness effects on black hole entropy spectra, or loop quantum cosmology's 'bounce' replacing the Big Bang singularity (potentially observable in CMB anomalies). Current data are consistent with no deviation from GR/QFT, which constrains but has not yet ruled in or out LQG; further tests are feasible but not yet decisive."

Loop quantum gravity is E2: a mathematically rigorous, actively researched quantum-gravity program with a genuine derived result (discrete area/volume spectra) but no experimental confirmation and unresolved issues recovering classical GR. It is a direct rival to string theory for the same explanatory niche, and the two communities dispute which (if either) is on the right track — a live tension worth flagging for the domain overview. Any claim that "space is made of discrete quanta of consciousness" or similar borrows LQG's discreteness result as a metaphor; LQG itself makes no claim about consciousness.
