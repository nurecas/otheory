strongest_objection: "The claim as stated — that a 2-D circle diagram literally encodes the structural design of every atom, molecule, and life form — has no supporting mechanism and is not derived from or tested against actual physics or biology; it substitutes visual resemblance and assertion for demonstrated causal or structural connection. No peer-reviewed physics or biology finds atomic or molecular structure 'derived from' the Flower of Life. This is the same failure pattern documented for the golden-ratio-ubiquity claim (Markowsky 1992): cherry-picked resemblance dressed as a universal law. No mechanism is proposed, no measurable quantity is specified, and nothing distinguishes confirming from disconfirming cases."
