Sacred geometry as an actual, cross-cultural symbolic and artistic practice is honestly filed at E5 (symbolic/contemplative), on the same footing as other traditions' cosmologies in this corpus — meaningful as symbol and practice, not as empirical or physical claim. This is precisely the register O Theory's "sacred geometry" imagery should occupy when it is being used evocatively (visual motifs, contemplative resonance): a real tradition, using real mathematics (Platonic solids, tessellations, regular polygons) toward symbolic ends, clearly separated from the E6 claim that such geometry proves or encodes cosmic design.
