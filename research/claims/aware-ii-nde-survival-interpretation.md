steelman: "Proponents argue that if lucid, structured, and morally-evaluative experiences are genuinely occurring during periods when standard clinical criteria would predict insufficient brain function to support them, this is at minimum an anomaly for a strict brain-produces-mind identity theory, and is consistent with (though not proof of) a view on which the brain filters or localizes a consciousness that is not wholly generated by it. Parnia and colleagues have explicitly floated 'new dimensions of reality' language and non-standard accounts of mind-brain relation in interpreting these results."
strongest_objection: "No AWARE-II data establishes that reported experiences occurred during a period of confirmed zero brain activity: EEG monitoring with interpretable signal was available for only 53 of 567 patients, the timing of recalled content relative to physiological state was not established, and the one objective test of perception during arrest (implicit auditory learning) returned a near-chance result (1/28 = 3.5%, and 0/28 on the visual-image test), i.e., failed to support the claim that veridical perception was occurring during arrest. Standard neuroscience has well-developed (if incomplete) accounts of DMT/glutamate surges, disinhibition, and hypoxia-related cortical hyperactivity producing vivid, memory-laden, emotionally intense experiences without invoking non-local mind, and these are the mechanisms the study's own authors propose. The 'survival' or strong non-locality reading goes well beyond what any controlled measurement in this study shows, and no experiment has been proposed that would distinguish 'non-local consciousness operating independent of the brain' from 'residual or rapidly-recovering brain function producing rich retrospective narrative,' making the interpretation not empirically decidable — E4."
falsifiability: "Not decidable with current or foreseeable methods: absent a way to independently timestamp and verify perceptual content during a period of rigorously confirmed zero cortical activity (which by definition is difficult to achieve safely in living subjects), the non-local/survival reading cannot be distinguished from a residual-or-rebound-brain-function account. Classified E4."
