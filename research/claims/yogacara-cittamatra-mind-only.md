Yogācāra ("practice of yoga") Buddhism, associated with Asaṅga and Vasubandhu, holds that the world of apparent external objects is vijñapti-mātra or cittamātra — "representation-only" or "mind-only" — arising from transformations of consciousness, including a substrate "storehouse consciousness" (ālayavijñāna). Represented here as Yogācāra's own doctrinal claim (E5), distinct from Madhyamaka emptiness with which it is sometimes conflated in popular accounts. Any mapping onto modern philosophical idealism or an O-Theory "consciousness-as-substrate" framing is an analogy at best, not a demonstrated identity.
