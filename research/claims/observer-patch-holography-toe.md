steelman: "Observer Patch Holography (OPH) is a comparatively disciplined self-published proposal. Its animating idea is a real and respectable theme in quantum foundations: that no observer accesses a global state, that each holds a finite 'patch,' and that objective physics is whatever survives as a consensus fixed point across overlapping observers — a stance with genuine kinship to relational quantum mechanics, QBism, and the holographic-screen literature. Unlike pure numerology, the repository states explicit fixed-point equations, distinguishes 'source-only' predictions from empirically fitted inputs, is candid that no theory is proven, and — most creditably — publishes ~40 concrete falsification pathways (massive gravitons, gauge-mediated proton decay, a fourth light generation, charge-lattice outliers) that would kill it. It reproduces uncontroversial structural facts (massless photon/gluon/graviton as structural zeroes; the SM gauge group and its Z6 quotient) and frames E8 and icosahedral (A5) symmetry as regulators rather than mysticism."
strongest_objection: "It presents as a complete physics derivation yet has no peer review, no independent replication, and displays the classic warning signs of fit-driven numerology dressed as first-principles derivation. Its headline 'predictions' match measured constants to absurd precision — the inverse fine-structure constant to ~0.00003σ (137.03595950081728 vs 137.035999177) and the Higgs mass to ~0.05% — a degree of agreement no honest first-principles TOE achieves for α, whose value is not derivable from any accepted theory; such matches almost always indicate parameters tuned (consciously or not) to known targets. The dimensional 'derivation' (e.g. 6−3=3 for space) and the invocation of A5/E8 as 'anchors' are asserted, not shown to be forced by the framework. Distler-Garibaldi-type embedding obstructions that sank Lisi's E8 model are not addressed. Absent a refereed derivation an independent physicist can reproduce, and given the retrofit-to-known-constants pattern, it does not clear the bar of established or even live-minority physics."
falsifiability: "The repository itself lists killing tests (a nonzero graviton mass, gauge-mediated proton decay, a fourth light matter generation, neutrino data excluding its branch), which is to its credit and is why it is not mere word-salad. But the decisive weakness is that its confirmations are postdictions of already-measured constants (α, Higgs mass, the SM gauge group) rather than a novel quantity measured after prediction; until an independent, refereed reproduction derives a not-yet-measured value that later checks out, the framework cannot be distinguished from parameter-fitting and remains E6."

Observer Patch Holography (OPH), authored under the handle FloatingPragma and hosted on GitHub (github.com/FloatingPragma/observer-patch-holography; repository confirmed live, ~551 stars, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, no peer review), is a self-published, non-peer-reviewed "theory of everything." Its core premise — physics as the public fixed point that survives agreement across overlapping finite observer "patches" — is a coherent and even interesting reframing that echoes relational QM, QBism, and holographic-screen ideas. The repository is unusually transparent for the genre: it states fixed-point equations, separates "source-only" predictions from fitted values, and publishes a falsifiability map of ~40 explicit OPH-killing pathways (all verified present in the repo). On that basis it is a more careful artifact than typical fringe TOEs, and the steelman credits this. However, it is filed E6 (presents-as-science, fails vetting) rather than E3, because tier is about vetting status, not politeness: it has no peer review or independent replication, and its headline results are digit-matching postdictions of already-known constants — the inverse fine-structure constant to ~0.00003σ (137.03595950081728 vs PDG 137.035999177(21), confirmed in the README) and the Higgs mass to ~0.05% — which is the hallmark signature of fitting to known targets rather than genuine first-principles derivation (the fine-structure constant is not derivable from any accepted theory). Its use of E8 and icosahedral (A5) symmetry as "anchors" is asserted, not shown to be forced, and it does not engage the Distler-Garibaldi embedding obstructions that refuted Lisi's E8 model (lisi-e8-toe, itself E3-disputed).
