steelman: "Using contrastive paradigms (same stimulus, seen vs. unseen), decades of fMRI, EEG, MEG, and intracranial work have converged on a reproducible finding: conscious perception correlates most reliably with sustained activity in a posterior cortical 'hot zone' (temporal, parietal, occipital association areas), while prefrontal involvement tracks report/task demands more than perception per se. This convergence held up under the adversarial 2025 COGITATE test, which is unusually strong replication for cognitive neuroscience."
strongest_objection: "NCC findings are correlational, not explanatory: they identify where and when neural activity tracks reported experience, not why that activity is experience rather than unconscious processing accompanying it. The field also depends on subjective report as ground truth, which is contested (no-report paradigms are still immature), and 'minimal sufficient mechanism' has never been established for any content — only correlation across conditions. Hence NCC results are solid empirical science (E1-adjacent) but do not by themselves adjudicate between rival theories (GNWT, IIT) of why those correlates constitute consciousness, which remains E2/E3 territory."
falsifiability: "Individual NCC claims are falsifiable and routinely revised (e.g., COGITATE's failure to find IIT-predicted sustained posterior gamma synchrony, or GNWT-predicted offset ignition). The broader program is progressively testable via no-report paradigms, intracranial recording, and perturbational methods (e.g., TMS-EEG complexity index), and is judged E2 here because the general existence of consistent neural correlates is well established, while the specific proposed 'markers' remain in active revision rather than settled fact."

This is an observation-level claim about the empirical NCC research program itself, distinct from any single theory's interpretation of the correlates (GNWT and IIT are filed separately). The program's core finding — reproducible neural signatures that track conscious report, concentrated posteriorly — is real science; whether those correlates are the seat, product, or filter of consciousness is not addressed here. For O Theory's produced-vs-localized question, NCC results constrain what any "receiver" or "generator" theory must explain but do not by themselves favor either framing.
