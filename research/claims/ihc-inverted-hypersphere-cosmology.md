It presents as physics but fails standard vetting."
falsifiability: "As stated, IHC is effectively unfalsifiable as a physical theory: its quantitative claims are retrodictions of already-known constants, so agreement with them provides no discriminating test. To count as physics it would need to state a NEW quantity (not yet measured) that it predicts to specified precision and that a feasible experiment or observation could contradict — e.g. a specific deviation in CMB topology signatures for RP4/non-trivial cosmic topology, which current all-sky searches (Planck) have NOT found. Absent such a prediction, the framework is reclassified as metaphysical/pseudoscientific rather than empirical."

Inverted Hypersphere Cosmology (Peacock, Samuel & Hall, Lauren; Zenodo preprint DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20596985, v10, 8 June 2026; record confirmed, resource type "Preprint," no peer review) attempts to derive fundamental physics from one metaphysical axiom — 'the logical instability of non-existence' (a state of nothing is not self-consistent) — claiming this, plus self-consistency and the CPT theorem, forces a CPT-invariant de Sitter vacuum with RP4 topology and yields the fine-structure constant (137.036), the weak mixing angle (sin^2 theta_W = 3*phi^-1/8), the dark-energy fraction (0.6882), phi-power lepton masses, and a shell number N=33 (all confirmed present in the abstract). It is discussed by a small online community (subreddit r/IHCcosmology), which is context only and not a citable source; the citable record is the Zenodo preprint. Assessment: self-published, non-peer-reviewed, no institutional affiliation or visible references, and its 'predictions' are post-hoc numerological fits to constants already measured — the golden-ratio-for-alpha genre in particular is a known coincidence-fitting pattern (Sherbon's PhilArchive papers, verified, exemplify the genre). With no novel confirmed prediction and an untestable founding axiom, it presents as science but fails vetting: tier E6, status rejected. NOT to be used as support anywhere in the synthesis; recorded for transparency and honest representation of fringe work. Verification note: the Sherbon URL philarchive.org/rec/SHEGRG resolves to Sherbon's "Golden Ratio Geometry and the Fine-Structure Constant" (a real, distinct-but-cognate paper to his "Fine-Structure Constant from Golden Ratio Geometry"); it validly documents the genre and is marked verified:true.
