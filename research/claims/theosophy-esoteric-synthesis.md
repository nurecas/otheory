Theosophy is E5: a real, historically important modern esoteric movement, honestly dated to its 1875 founding and represented as Blavatsky's own synthesis rather than as verified ancient doctrine. It is the direct ancestor of much of the vocabulary ("vibration," "planes," "root races," cosmic evolution) that recurs across later New Age and pseudoscientific "energy" claims — relevant to O Theory as the historical source of much of the aesthetic it must keep separate from any physics claim.

**Red-team notes (2026-07-07):** Both sources verified directly against their content; the 1875 New York founding, the three named founders, and the characterization of Theosophy as fundamentally a Western synthesis with an Eastern veneer are all accurately represented. Worth flagging for the broader corpus: the chakra-source review for this same vetting pass turned up an independent confirmation that the modern Western chakra system itself was substantially transmitted via Theosophy (Leadbeater 1927, The Chakras, Theosophical Publishing House, cited within Moga 2022) — i.e., this claim's note that Theosophy is "the direct ancestor of much of the vocabulary... that recurs across later New Age and pseudoscientific 'energy' claims" is not just plausible but specifically corroborated by the citation trail in the chakra-measurability claim. Tier E5 confirmed — a real, well-documented historical movement, honestly dated and attributed to Blavatsky's own synthesis rather than treated as recovered ancient doctrine. Status set to vetted.
