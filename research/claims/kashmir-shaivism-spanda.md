Kashmir Śaivism (Trika), especially its Spanda school, holds that the sole reality is a unitary consciousness, Śiva, inseparable from its own dynamic power, Śakti, and that this consciousness's essential nature is spanda — a subtle, self-referential pulsation or vibration of awareness (prakāśa, luminosity, united with vimarśa, self-reflective activity) through which the universe is manifested and reabsorbed. Represented here strictly as this tradition's own contemplative-metaphysical claim (E5); the term "vibration" is technical/symbolic within this tradition and must not be conflated with physical vibration, wave mechanics, or any O-Theory "field" language beyond loose metaphor.
