steelman: "Carhart-Harris et al. (2012) found psilocybin acutely decreased blood flow and coordinated activity within DMN hub regions (medial prefrontal cortex, posterior cingulate), and the magnitude of DMN disruption correlated with subjective 'ego dissolution' ratings; the 2016 LSD study specifically linked reduced parahippocampal-retrosplenial connectivity to self-reported ego dissolution using multimodal imaging (fMRI, MEG). The 2022/2023 systematic review (Gattuso et al.) found consistent acute DMN connectivity disruption and increased between-network connectivity replicated across psilocybin, LSD, and ayahuasca studies, making the DMN-modulation pattern one of the more consistent findings in the neuroimaging-of-altered-states literature."
strongest_objection: "Correlation between DMN disruption and self-reported ego dissolution does not establish that DMN activity constitutes or causes the sense of self, nor that reduced DMN coherence is the same neural event across psilocybin, LSD, meditation, and other altered states — the DMN-ego link is a hypothesis (the 'entropic brain'/relaxed-beliefs framework) built on correlational neuroimaging, not a demonstrated causal mechanism. The systematic review itself is notably cautious on this exact point: it explicitly states it is 'unclear how central the DMN is to the therapeutic potential' of psychedelics, and flags that benefits may derive from multiple mechanisms (including neuroplasticity and cellular-level changes) beyond DMN modulation — the claim file's confidence should not exceed the review's own hedging. Sample sizes in most individual studies are small (typically n=10-25) and self-report of 'ego dissolution' is itself a construct requiring its own validated scale (e.g., the Ego-Dissolution Inventory), adding a further layer of measurement uncertainty. None of this bears on whether a 'dissolved ego' encounters anything real."
falsifiability: "Empirical/neuroimaging claim: falsifiable by failure to replicate the DMN-connectivity/ego-dissolution-rating correlation, or by finding the correlation is better explained by a confound (e.g., general arousal, motion artifact under altered perception). Multiple independent studies and a 2022/2023 systematic review support the correlation, though causal mechanism claims remain more tentative (E3, not E1)."

This is an EMPIRICAL/NEUROSCIENCE claim (E3): a real, replicated (though modest-N, correlational) association between reduced DMN integration and self-reported "ego dissolution" under psychedelics. It is strictly a brain-correlate-of-a-self-report finding, not a demonstration that the self is "really" absent or that anything beyond the self is contacted when DMN activity drops — those are separate metaphysical readings (E4), addressed in mystical-experience-epistemic-status. In O Theory's terms, DMN attenuation is the best-characterized neural correlate candidate for a "widened valve," strictly as a brain-state correlate of a reported experience, not as evidence of what (if anything) is let through.
