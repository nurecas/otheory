steelman: "Plotinus (c. 204-270 CE) argues that all multiplicity must derive from an absolutely simple, unitary first principle beyond being and thought itself, 'the One,' from which Intellect (Nous) and then Soul emanate by a kind of overflow (not creation ex nihilo or diminishment of the source), with the material world as the furthest, faintest stage; the soul's return (henosis) to the One via contemplation is the goal of philosophy. This is a rigorously argued metaphysical system foundational to later Western mysticism (Christian, Jewish, and Islamic)."

Its influence on Augustine, Pseudo-Dionysius, and Islamic Neoplatonism means later traditions build on it while also revising it substantially."

Plotinus's Enneads describe reality as a hierarchy emanating from "the One" (to hen) — an absolutely simple, ineffable first principle beyond being, thought, and multiplicity — through Intellect (Nous, the realm of Forms) and Soul, down to the material world, with the human soul capable of ascending back toward unity with the One through contemplation. Represented here as Neoplatonism's own philosophical-contemplative claim (E5/E4); its historical influence on Christian, Jewish, and Islamic mysticism (via Augustine, Pseudo-Dionysius, and Islamic Neoplatonists) is well documented, but any O-Theory mapping of "the One" onto an "O-Source" is metaphor/analogy, not shared mathematics or evidence.
