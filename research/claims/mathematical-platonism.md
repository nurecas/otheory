Mathematical Platonism is a live, seriously defended position in analytic philosophy of mathematics (not a fringe view), but it is a metaphysical thesis about the ontological status of abstracta, not an empirical claim — hence E4. It bears directly on O Theory's appeals to "mathematical structure" or "information" as bedrock: if numbers/structures are mind-independently real, a structural or informational substrate is at least intelligible as fundamental; if Platonism is false (structures are useful fictions or mental constructs), any claim that "reality just is mathematics" loses its ontological footing and collapses to a claim about description, not being. Observation vs. interpretation: that physics is highly successfully described by mathematics is uncontroversial; that mathematical objects therefore exist mind-independently is the separate, contested Platonist interpretation.
