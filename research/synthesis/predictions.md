# Predictions — falsifiable, with tests

_Owned by the `synthesizer` agent. Seed version (Stage 2)._

A prediction only belongs here if something could confirm or falsify it. Ideas that nothing could falsify
are **not** listed as predictions — they live in the philosophical (E4) or symbolic (E5) layers instead.
Each entry names the tier of the theory generating it; a passed test does **not** promote the theory's tier
by itself.
