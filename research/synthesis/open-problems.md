# Open Problems — the honest ledger

_Owned by the `synthesizer` agent. Synthesis-first version (2026-07-07). The gaps are a feature, not an
embarrassment — they are what an honest map looks like at 132 claims._

1. **The measurement problem is unresolved.** Decoherence (`qm-decoherence`, E1) explains why we see
   classical outcomes but not why a *single* outcome is experienced. Every interpretation — many-worlds (E2),
   Bohm (E2), GRW/CSL (E2), relational QM (E2), consistent histories (E2), Copenhagen (E2) — reproduces the
   same statistics (`measurement-problem-statement`, E1). *What would move it:* an experiment distinguishing
   the interpretations — most concretely, an objective-collapse deviation from unitary QM, which GRW/CSL is
   actively being constrained against.

2. **There is no confirmed physical substrate of experience.** COGITATE 2025
   (`cogitate-adversarial-collaboration-2025`, E1 as a study) vindicated **neither** IIT
   (`iit-integrated-information`) **nor** GNWT (`gnwt-global-workspace`) — both were partially disconfirmed,
   and both remain disputed. Orch-OR (`orch-or-microtubules`) still faces Tegmark's decoherence-timescale
   objection; the 2025 microtubule-anesthesia work (`wiest-anesthesia-microtubule-observation`) is suggestive,
   not confirmatory, and leans partly on a contested entanglement claim. *What would move it:* direct evidence
   of functionally relevant quantum coherence in neurons, or a theory whose neural predictions survive the next
   adversarial test.

3. **The decombination problem.** Cosmopsychism and the O-Source (`cosmopsychism`, E4), analytic idealism
   (`analytic-idealism-kastrup`, E4), and the experience-first metaphysics carried by the Nameless project
   (`nameless-experience-first-metaphysics`, E4) must all explain how one cosmic subject or experiential ground
   yields many bounded private subjects without simply reintroducing the hard problem. Kastrup's dissociation
   analogy arguably relocates rather than solves it, and the other views inherit the same gap. This is the
   mirror of panpsychism's combination problem (`panpsychism-combination-problem`). *What would move it:* a
   rigorous account of how one subject individuates into many — but this is philosophy (E4), not something
   evidence can settle.

4. **The quantum-to-consciousness bridge rests on a single, weak claim.** The only claim in the corpus that
   literally makes a mind collapse the wavefunction is von Neumann–Wigner
   (`von-neumann-wigner-consciousness-collapse`, **E3, disputed**). Decoherence independently dissolves its
   original motivation; the quantum Zeno effect already falsifies its naive versions (Chalmers & McQueen 2022);
   and de Barros & Oas (2017) argue that its surviving versions are structurally near-unfalsifiable. The physics
   "observer" is a measurement interaction, **not** a mind (the `observer-dependence` bridge, ceiling E3). Any
   move from "observation matters in QM" to "consciousness is fundamental to QM" therefore hangs on this one
   disputed E3 claim, and must be labeled as such. *What would move it:* a detected consciousness-dependent
   effect on a quantum outcome — none has ever been observed.

5. **Observation is not interpretation — a distinction that recurs.** The corpus deliberately splits each
   finding from its metaphysical reading, and the gap is real in every case:
   - *AI:* the bliss attractor (`claude-spiritual-bliss-attractor-observation`, E3) and concept-injection
     introspection (`anthropic-introspective-awareness-concept-injection`, E3) are output and activation
     statistics; the sentience and introspection readings (`spiritual-bliss-machine-sentience-interpretation`,
     `llm-introspection-genuine-awareness-interpretation`, both E4) are equally well explained by the
     RLHF-persona account. *No validated marker of machine experience exists.*
   - *NDEs:* AWARE-II lucid recall (`aware-ii-nde-phenomenology`, E3) had a near-chance objective test (1 of 28);
     the survival and non-local reading (`aware-ii-nde-survival-interpretation`, E4) goes well beyond it.
   - *Terminal lucidity:* the reports are real (`terminal-lucidity-observation`, E3); the filter reading
     (`terminal-lucidity-filter-interpretation`, E4) makes no distinct testable prediction — "the filter
     loosened" and "spared circuits transiently recovered" predict the same data.
   *What would move each:* an observable that the interpretation predicts and its materialist rival does not.

6. **Illusionism is the sharpest rival to the whole family.** `illusionism-eliminativism` (E4) holds that there
   is no phenomenal consciousness to explain, only a robust *representation* of one. If it is right, the hard
   problem dissolves and the entire consciousness-is-fundamental program — including O Theory — loses its
   motivation. It is not a fringe position; it is the strongest opposing E4. The synthesis must present it as a
   live rival, not a footnote. *What would move it:* nothing empirical, since this is argued at E4 — but the
   unresolved "explain the illusion" challenge is where the debate actually turns.

7. **The traditions do not all agree.** "They all point at the O-Source" is the perennial-philosophy common-core
   thesis (`perennial-philosophy-common-core-thesis`, **E4, disputed**), not a fact. Madhyamaka's śūnyatā
   (`madhyamaka-sunyata-emptiness`, E5) **explicitly resists** reification into any "ground of being" or "One" —
   the tradition declares emptiness itself empty precisely to block that move — so it cannot be folded in with
   Brahman, the One, and Ein Sof without misrepresentation. Katz's constructivism holds that the experiences are
   shaped differently all the way down. *What would move it:* nothing decisive — we have only already-interpreted
   reports to compare (`perennialism-constructivism-debate`, E4) — which is exactly why it stays a contested
   comparative thesis.

8. **The PSR-regress applies to O Theory's own O-Source.** The Principle of Sufficient Reason
   (`principle-of-sufficient-reason`, E4) demands a reason for every fact — including the O-Source itself. If the
   O-Source needs no explanation, then so might the physical world (no O-Source required); if it does need one,
   the regress is not stopped. This objection is filed against O Theory's own ground, not only against rival
   substrates. The same fairness applies to the Nameless project's "nothing must distinguish itself" argument
   (`nameless-experience-first-metaphysics`, E4): that we cannot conceive an undifferentiated void without
   importing a contrast shows a limit of representation, not that reality's nothing is compelled to become
   something.

9. **Psychometric circularity in mysticism.** The MEQ30 (`meq30-mystical-experience-psychometrics`) and the
   M-Scale (`hood-mysticism-scale-psychometrics`) are real, replicated E3 instruments — but their items were
   written from Stace's categories (`stace-common-core-typology`, E5), so finding "Stace-shaped" experiences
   partly reflects the questionnaire's own design. The DMN correlations (`meditation-dmn-neuroscience`,
   `psychedelic-ego-dissolution-dmn`) are cross-sectional and correlational, with self-selection confounds, and
   the psilocybin RCTs (`griffiths-psilocybin-mystical-experience`) suffer functional unblinding. *What would
   move it:* instruments derived independently of Stace's typology, and blinded or longitudinal designs.

10. **The 2025 LLM observations have not been independently replicated.** Both the bliss attractor and the
    concept-injection results are **single-lab (Anthropic) and unreplicated**. Until a non-Anthropic group
    reproduces them, they are provisional even as *observations*. *What would move it:* independent replication
    of the feature-steering and concept-detection effects.

11. **The free-energy principle's unfalsifiability.** In its most general form the FEP
    (`predictive-processing-free-energy-principle`, E2, disputed) faces the "dark room" objection: if organisms
    minimize prediction error, why not seek a dark, silent, unchanging room? A framework that can absorb any
    behavior is unfalsifiable. *What would move it:* a specification of the principle that forbids some observable
    behavior.

12. **The competing metaphysics are rivals, not complements.** Information-first
    (`it-from-bit-information-ontology`, `pancomputationalism-digital-physics`, the MUH), process-first
    (`process-philosophy-actual-occasions`), structure-first (`ontic-structural-realism`), and Platonism
    (`mathematical-platonism`) are **mutually exclusive** answers to "what is fundamental." O Theory cannot lean
    on all of them at once, and the synthesis must not present them as a harmonious stack when they are
    competitors — each with its own decisive objection (Newman's problem, the description-versus-thing-described
    conflation, Benacerraf's access problem).

13. **A family of "seed" theories of everything — real mathematics, unlicensed conclusions, mostly unreviewed.**
    A recognizable family of recent proposals tries to grow all of physics from a single geometric or
    informational seed, and they are weighed here evenly, with no member singled out: Lisi's E8 model
    (`lisi-e8-toe`, E3) from an exceptional Lie group; Nielsen's Topological Unified Field Theory
    (`nielsen-topological-unified-field-theory`, E3) from a complex Hopf fibration; the Nameless project
    (nurecas.com) (`nameless-qubit-standard-model-derivation`, E3) from a single qubit's geometry; and, failing
    vetting outright, IHC (`ihc-inverted-hypersphere-cosmology`, E6), Observer Patch Holography
    (`observer-patch-holography-toe`, E6), and Haramein's holographic-mass model
    (`haramein-schwarzschild-proton-holographic-mass`, E6). Two tensions run through the whole family. First, the
    underlying mathematics is often genuinely E1 — the Hopf fibration and the CP∞ bundle classification are
    settled topology (`hopf-fibration-topology-physics`, `u1-bundles-classified-by-cp-infinity`) — but the
    *unification conclusion* does not follow from it: the classifying-space theorem constrains only abelian U(1)
    bundles and privileges no particular finite stage, so deriving a specific spacetime, the full non-abelian
    Standard Model, and gravity is an unlicensed step. Second, matching small-integer or golden-ratio values
    (sin²θ_W = 3/8 is the known SU(5) value; 4/13 ≈ 0.3077; golden-ratio fits to α) risks **numerology** — with
    enough freedom in how modes map to particle content, there are many simple expressions near any target, so a
    fit is not a mechanism. These works are largely self-published (viXra, Zenodo, GitHub, a young non-Scopus
    journal) and not independently reviewed, which is why the credentialed ones sit at E3 and the rest at E6 —
    the line is drawn by author and venue, **not** by confirmation, and none is confirmed. *What would move it:*
    independent physicists checking the algebra (does it force the gauge content, or reverse-engineer it?), a
    refereed derivation of a not-yet-measured quantity, or an experiment — for example, JUNO deciding a predicted
    neutrino-mixing value around 2030 (see `predictions.md`), which is one such test among the family, not a
    verdict on the whole.

14. **The standing cross-register temptation.** The strongest pull in this whole area is to let an E5 metaphor
    ("all is one"), an E3 observation ("microtubules!", "the model introspects!"), an E3 unification proposal
    ("physics from a single seed!"), or an E2 result ("information reconstructs QM!") do E1 work. This ledger
    exists partly to keep naming that pull. The register-and-ceiling label on every bridge, the
    observation-versus-interpretation split, and the deliberate separation of genuine E1 mathematics from the E3
    conclusions drawn from it are the mechanisms that catch it before publish.
