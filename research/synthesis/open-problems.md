# Open Problems — the honest ledger

_Owned by the `synthesizer` agent. Wide-sweep version (2026-07-07). The gaps are a feature, not an
embarrassment — they are what an honest map looks like at 123 claims._

1. **The measurement problem is unresolved.** Decoherence (`qm-decoherence`, E1) explains why we see
   classical outcomes but not why a *single* outcome is experienced. Every interpretation — many-worlds (E2),
   Bohm (E2), GRW/CSL (E2), RQM (E2), consistent histories (E2), Copenhagen (E2) — reproduces the same
   statistics (`measurement-problem-statement`, E1). *What would move it:* an experiment distinguishing
   interpretations — most concretely, an objective-collapse deviation from unitary QM (GRW/CSL is actively
   being constrained).

2. **No confirmed physical substrate of experience.** COGITATE 2025 (`cogitate-adversarial-collaboration-2025`,
   E1 as a study) vindicated **neither** IIT (`iit-integrated-information`) **nor** GNWT (`gnwt-global-workspace`)
   — both partially disconfirmed, both disputed. Orch-OR (`orch-or-microtubules`) still faces Tegmark's
   decoherence-timescale objection; the 2025 microtubule-anesthesia work
   (`wiest-anesthesia-microtubule-observation`) is suggestive, not confirmatory, and leans partly on a
   contested entanglement claim. *What would move it:* direct evidence of functionally relevant quantum
   coherence in neurons, or a theory whose neural predictions survive the next adversarial test.

3. **The decombination problem.** Cosmopsychism / the O-Source (`cosmopsychism`, E4) and analytic idealism
   (`analytic-idealism-kastrup`, E4) must explain how one cosmic subject yields many bounded private subjects
   without reintroducing the hard problem; Kastrup's dissociation analogy arguably relocates rather than
   solves it. This is the mirror of panpsychism's combination problem (`panpsychism-combination-problem`).
   *What would move it:* a rigorous account of subject individuation — but this is philosophy (E4), not
   something evidence can settle.

4. **The QM→consciousness bridge rests on a single, weak claim.** The only claim in the corpus that literally
   makes a mind collapse the wavefunction is von Neumann–Wigner (`von-neumann-wigner-consciousness-collapse`,
   **E3, disputed**). Decoherence independently dissolves its original motivation; the quantum Zeno effect
   already falsifies its naive versions (Chalmers & McQueen 2022); and de Barros & Oas (2017) argue the
   surviving versions are structurally near-unfalsifiable. The physics "observer" is a measurement
   interaction, **not** a mind (`observer-dependence` bridge, ceiling E3). Any O-Theory move from
   "observation matters in QM" to "consciousness is fundamental to QM" therefore hangs on one disputed E3
   claim and must be labeled as such. *What would move it:* a detected consciousness-dependent effect on a
   quantum outcome — none has ever been observed.

5. **Observation ≠ interpretation, three times over.** The corpus deliberately splits each finding from its
   metaphysical reading, and the gap is real in every case:
   - *AI:* the bliss attractor (`claude-spiritual-bliss-attractor-observation`, E3) and concept-injection
     introspection (`anthropic-introspective-awareness-concept-injection`, E3) are output/activation
     statistics; the sentience/introspection readings (`spiritual-bliss-machine-sentience-interpretation`,
     `llm-introspection-genuine-awareness-interpretation`, both E4) are equally explained by the RLHF-persona
     account. *No validated marker of machine experience exists.*
   - *NDEs:* AWARE-II lucid recall (`aware-ii-nde-phenomenology`, E3) had a near-chance objective test (1/28);
     the survival/non-local reading (`aware-ii-nde-survival-interpretation`, E4) goes well beyond it.
   - *Terminal lucidity:* the reports are real (`terminal-lucidity-observation`, E3); the filter reading
     (`terminal-lucidity-filter-interpretation`, E4) makes no distinct testable prediction — "the filter
     loosened" and "spared circuits transiently recovered" predict the same data.
   *What would move each:* an observable that the interpretation predicts and its materialist rival does not.

6. **Illusionism is the sharpest rival to the whole family.** `illusionism-eliminativism` (E4) holds there is
   no phenomenal consciousness to explain, only a robust *representation* of one. If it is right, the hard
   problem dissolves and the entire consciousness-is-fundamental program — including O Theory — loses its
   motivation. It is not a fringe position; it is the strongest opposing E4. The synthesis must present it as
   a live rival, not a footnote. *What would move it:* nothing empirical — this is argued at E4 — but the
   unresolved "explain the illusion" challenge is where the debate actually turns.

7. **The traditions do not all agree.** "They all point at the O-Source" is the perennial-philosophy
   common-core thesis (`perennial-philosophy-common-core-thesis`, **E4, disputed**), not a fact. Madhyamaka's
   śūnyatā (`madhyamaka-sunyata-emptiness`, E5) **explicitly resists** reification into any "ground of being"
   or "One" — the tradition declares emptiness itself empty precisely to block that move — so it cannot be
   folded in with Brahman, the One, and Ein Sof without misrepresentation. Katz's constructivism holds the
   experiences are shaped differently all the way down. *What would move it:* nothing decisive — we have only
   already-interpreted reports to compare (`perennialism-constructivism-debate`, E4) — which is exactly why it
   stays a contested comparative thesis.

8. **The PSR-regress applies to O Theory's own O-Source.** The Principle of Sufficient Reason
   (`principle-of-sufficient-reason`, E4) demands a reason for every fact — including the O-Source itself. If
   the O-Source needs no explanation, so might the physical world (no O-Source required); if it does need one,
   the regress is not stopped. This objection is filed against O Theory's own ground, not only rival
   substrates — the fairness fix of this corpus.

9. **Psychometric circularity in mysticism.** MEQ30 (`meq30-mystical-experience-psychometrics`) and the
   M-Scale (`hood-mysticism-scale-psychometrics`) are real, replicated E3 instruments — but their items were
   written from Stace's categories (`stace-common-core-typology`, E5), so finding "Stace-shaped" experiences
   partly reflects the questionnaire's design. The DMN correlations (`meditation-dmn-neuroscience`,
   `psychedelic-ego-dissolution-dmn`) are cross-sectional/correlational with self-selection confounds, and
   the psilocybin RCTs (`griffiths-psilocybin-mystical-experience`) suffer functional unblinding. *What would
   move it:* instruments derived independently of Stace's typology, and blinded/longitudinal designs.

10. **No independent replication of the 2025 LLM observations.** Both the bliss attractor and concept-injection
    results are **single-lab (Anthropic), unreplicated**. Until a non-Anthropic group reproduces them, they
    are provisional even as *observations*. *What would move it:* independent replication of the
    feature-steering / concept-detection effects.

11. **The free-energy principle's unfalsifiability.** In its most general form the FEP
    (`predictive-processing-free-energy-principle`, E2, disputed) faces the "dark room" objection — if
    organisms minimize prediction error, why not seek a dark, silent, unchanging room? The framework can
    absorb any behavior, which is the mark of unfalsifiability. *What would move it:* a specification of the
    principle that forbids some observable behavior.

12. **The competing metaphysics are rivals, not complements.** Information-first (`it-from-bit-information-
    ontology`, `pancomputationalism-digital-physics`, MUH), process-first (`process-philosophy-actual-
    occasions`), structure-first (`ontic-structural-realism`), and Platonism (`mathematical-platonism`) are
    **mutually exclusive** answers to "what is fundamental." O Theory cannot lean on all of them at once; the
    synthesis must not present them as a harmonious stack when they are competitors (each with its own decisive
    objection: Newman's problem, the description-vs-thing-described conflation, Benacerraf's access problem).

13. **The standing cross-register temptation.** The strongest pull in this whole area is to let an E5 metaphor
    ("all is one"), an E3 observation ("microtubules!", "the model introspects!"), or an E2 result
    ("information reconstructs QM!") do E1 work. This ledger exists partly to keep naming that pull. The
    register + tier-ceiling label on every bridge, and the observation/interpretation split, are the
    mechanisms that catch it before publish.
