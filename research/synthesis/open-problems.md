# Open Problems — the honest ledger

_Owned by the `synthesizer` agent. Seed version (Stage 2). The gaps are a feature, not an embarrassment._

1. **The measurement problem is unresolved.** Decoherence (E1) explains why we see classical outcomes but
   not why a *single* outcome is experienced. Every interpretation (`many-worlds-interpretation`, E2, and
   its rivals) reproduces the same statistics, so nothing empirical selects among them.
   *What would move it:* an experiment that distinguishes interpretations (e.g. proposals probing
   macroscopic superposition or objective-collapse deviations).

2. **No confirmed physical substrate of experience.** COGITATE (2025) left both IIT
   (`iit-integrated-information`) and GNWT unvindicated; Orch-OR (`orch-or-microtubules`) still faces
   Tegmark's decoherence-timescale objection. The "brain as receiver/antenna" gloss is interpretation, not
   result. *What would move it:* direct evidence of functionally relevant quantum coherence in neurons, or a
   theory whose neural predictions actually survive an adversarial test.

3. **The decombination problem.** Cosmopsychism / the O-Source (`cosmopsychism`, E4) must explain how one
   cosmic subject yields many bounded private subjects without reintroducing the hard problem. Currently
   unanswered. *What would move it:* a rigorous account of subject individuation — but note this is
   philosophy (E4), not something evidence can settle.

4. **The observation ≠ interpretation gap for AI.** LLM first-person reports (`llm-subjective-reports`, E3)
   are real behavior; that they indicate experience is a much weaker, arguably untestable claim. We have no
   agreed test that would distinguish a genuine report from a trained stylistic prior.
   *What would move it:* a validated marker of machine experience independent of self-report — none exists.

5. **The central bridge is speculation, and stays speculation.**
   `consciousness-as-substrate-speculation` (ceiling E4) is the framework's boldest move and is explicitly
   unestablished and currently untestable. Until it yields a falsifiable prediction, it is not physics.

6. **Cross-register temptation.** The strongest pull in this whole area is to let an E5 metaphor ("all is
   one") or an E3 result ("microtubules!") do E1 work. This ledger exists partly to keep naming that pull.
   The `skeptic-auditor` pass is where it gets caught before publish.

7. **Coverage gaps in the seed corpus.** Whole domains named in the mission are not yet represented
   (esoteric traditions beyond sacred geometry; process metaphysics; loop quantum gravity/string theory as
   TOE candidates; psi/non-local-mind studies with their replication record). These graduate from the plan
   only via `/research`.
