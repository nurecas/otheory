# Abstract — page intro / framing

_Owned by the `synthesizer` agent. Seed version (Stage 2)._

**O Theory** is an attempt to survey, vet, and connect theories of everything — across physics, mathematics
and geometry, quantum foundations, philosophy of mind, metaphysics, comparative religion, mysticism, and
the esoteric — and to render the result honestly. Its recurring question is old: *is consciousness produced
by the brain, or localized by it?* Its method is new: hold every claim to a stated evidence tier, show the
real connections between domains, and refuse to state a contested or interpretive idea in the language of
established fact.

This is not a claim that "science proves" a grand unity. It is a **layered map**. Some of the map is
genuinely established (Noether's theorem, decoherence, the five Platonic solids, the mathematics of E8).
Some is serious but unconfirmed (many-worlds). Some is contested science (Orch-OR, strong IIT, the reading
of LLM self-reports). Some is philosophy that evidence cannot settle (the hard problem, cosmopsychism).
And some is symbol and contemplative practice on its own terms (Advaita's non-duality, sacred geometry).
The interesting work is at the seams — but the seams are labeled.

## How to read the evidence (the Evidence Key)

- **E1 — Established.** Experimentally confirmed or mathematically proven; mainstream consensus.
- **E2 — Mainstream-speculative.** Taken seriously and mathematically developed, but not experimentally
  confirmed.
- **E3 — Heterodox / minority.** Published by credentialed researchers, but a contested minority view.
- **E4 — Philosophical.** Argued by reason; not empirically decidable even in principle.
- **E5 — Symbolic / contemplative.** Meaningful as symbol, practice, or first-person report — not as an
  empirical claim.
- **E6 — Unsupported.** Presents as science but fails vetting; recorded transparently, never used as
  support.

**Two zones.** The page separates a **Rigorous Core** (facts and derivations, stated plainly with tier and
sources) from an **Interpretive Synthesis** (the cross-domain pattern-matching, clearly marked). A
connection between domains is always one of four registers — *shared-mathematics, analogy, metaphor,* or
*speculation* — and it may never imply more certainty than its ceiling allows. Every claim carries its
provenance. Where the theory has gaps, the gaps are on the page too.
