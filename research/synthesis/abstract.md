# Abstract — page intro / framing

_Owned by the `synthesizer` agent. Wide-sweep version (2026-07-07): 132 vetted claims across 10 domains._

**O Theory** is an attempt to survey, vet, and connect theories of everything — across physics, mathematics
and geometry, quantum foundations, consciousness science, philosophy of mind, metaphysics, comparative
religion, mysticism, the esoteric, and AI consciousness — and to render the result honestly. Its recurring
question is old: *is consciousness produced by the brain, or localized by it?* Its method is disciplined:
hold every claim to a stated evidence tier, split each observation from its interpretation, show the real
connections between domains, and refuse to state a contested or interpretive idea in the language of
established fact.

The corpus is now **132 vetted claims** — a 13-claim seed grown by a ten-domain sweep in which researchers
surveyed each field and independent vetters live-checked every source and set the tier. The distribution is
itself the honest headline: **E1: 23 · E2: 18 · E3: 21 · E4: 36 · E5: 23 · E6: 11**. Most of the map is
argued or symbolic, not proven — and it is drawn that way on purpose.

This is **not** a claim that "science proves" a grand unity. It is a **layered map**. Some of it is
genuinely established (general relativity, the Standard Model, the second law, the black-hole area law, Bell
and Kochen–Specker, Gödel, group theory, fractals, the five Platonic solids, E8, and the Hopf fibration with
its CP∞ classification of U(1) bundles — the genuine geometry of a qubit and of charge quantization). Some is
serious but unconfirmed (string/M-theory, loop quantum gravity, holography, the QM interpretations, the
free-energy principle). Some is contested science (Verlinde's gravity, Orch-OR, IIT, GNWT; a new wave of
"seed" theories of everything that try to grow physics from a geometric or informational germ — the Hopf
fibration as "canonical space," the Standard Model from a single qubit, one of them making a JUNO-testable
neutrino prediction; and the *observations* behind terminal lucidity, NDE recall, psychedelic ego-dissolution,
and the 2025 LLM "bliss attractor" and concept-injection results). Much of it is philosophy that evidence
cannot settle (the hard problem, panpsychism, idealism, illusionism, dual-aspect monism, Platonism, structural
realism, priority monism, and the Nameless project's experience-first metaphysics). And a large,
honestly-labeled part is symbol and contemplative practice on its own terms (Advaita, Madhyamaka, Plotinus,
the Dao, Kabbalah, Sufism, jhāna, sacred geometry, the Hermetic corpus). Eleven claims are rejected outright
(E6) and kept only for transparency — load-bearing for nothing. The interesting work is at the seams — but
the seams are labeled, and where the seams do not actually hold (the QM→consciousness bridge; the leap from
real Hopf/qubit mathematics to a proven unification; the claim that the traditions all agree), the page says
so.

Where does O Theory itself live? It is a **Layer-4 philosophical proposal** — a dual-aspect monism /
cosmopsychism, the position it names itself after — that reaches across the other layers by explicitly
labeled bridges, not by promotion. That placement is the whole point.

## How to read the evidence (the Evidence Key)

- **E1 — Established.** Experimentally confirmed or mathematically proven; mainstream consensus.
- **E2 — Mainstream-speculative.** Taken seriously and mathematically developed, but not experimentally
  confirmed.
- **E3 — Heterodox / minority.** Published by credentialed researchers, but a contested minority view.
- **E4 — Philosophical.** Argued by reason; not empirically decidable even in principle.
- **E5 — Symbolic / contemplative.** Meaningful as symbol, practice, or first-person report — not as an
  empirical claim.
- **E6 — Unsupported.** Presents as science but fails vetting; recorded transparently, never used as
  support.

An E5 label is not a demerit. It is an honest classification: symbolic material is included on its own terms,
never smuggled into physics.

**Two zones.** The page separates a **Rigorous Core** (facts and derivations, stated plainly with tier and
sources) from an **Interpretive Synthesis** (the cross-domain pattern-matching, clearly marked). A
connection between domains is always exactly one of four registers — *shared-mathematics, analogy, metaphor,*
or *speculation* — and it may never imply more certainty than its ceiling allows. A crucial discipline runs
through the whole synthesis: **observations are separated from their interpretations and tiered apart**, so
that a real E3 finding (a bliss attractor, a lucid episode, a null-adjacent NDE recall) never lends its
credibility to the E4 metaphysics someone reads into it — and, likewise, genuine E1 mathematics (the Hopf
fibration) never lends its standing to the E3 unification conclusion drawn from it. Every claim carries its
provenance. Where the theory has gaps, the gaps are on the page too.
