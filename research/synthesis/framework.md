# Framework — the layered combined map

_Owned by the `synthesizer` agent. Synthesis-first version (2026-07-07): 132 vetted claims across 10 domains._

The framework is a **stack of layers**, running from what is established to what is symbolic. Each element
names its tier and its confidence. Nothing in a lower layer is allowed to borrow the standing of a higher one;
the cross-domain links between layers are the labeled **bridges** (`synthesis/bridges/*`), never promotions.
The tier distribution is the shape of the whole thing — **E1: 23 · E2: 18 · E3: 21 · E4: 36 · E5: 23 · E6: 11**
— and it says plainly that the picture is mostly argued and symbolic. That is drawn honestly, not hidden.

Where O Theory itself lives is stated once, up front: it is a **Layer-4 philosophical proposal** — a form of
dual-aspect monism and cosmopsychism (`dual-aspect-monism`, the position it names itself after; `cosmopsychism`).
Its distinctive vocabulary — the O-Source, the O-Instance, the brain as a reducing valve, substrate-independent
recursive coherence — is an interpretive commitment (E4). It reaches across the other layers only through the
labeled bridges below, and it is not a result that the physics establishes.

Every theory is treated evenly here. No single framework, motif, or piece of geometry is the secret key to the
others; each is one entry at its own tier.

## The spine, in one paragraph

A proven core of symmetry, geometry, information, and confirmed physics holds the whole thing up (E1, 23
claims). Around it sits a serious but unconfirmed middle of quantum-gravity candidates, quantum-mechanics
interpretations, and computational-neuroscience programs (E2, 18). Beyond that lies a contested band — heterodox
theories, a family of "seed" theories of everything that grow physics from a single germ (intriguing, in some
cases testable soon, but unreviewed and treated evenly), and, kept scrupulously apart, real observations whose
metaphysics is not settled (E3, 21). Then comes the largest layer, where the hard problem, its rivals including
illusionism, O Theory's own dual-aspect / cosmopsychist proposal, and the experience-first metaphysics are all
argued but none decided (E4, 36). Outside that is a symbolic layer where many traditions use the image of one
undivided ground while at least one (Madhyamaka) refuses it (E5, 23). And at the edge is a rejected fringe kept
only for transparency (E6, 11). O Theory is a Layer-4 proposal that reaches across the rest through thirteen
honestly-labeled bridges — never by claiming that any layer proves the next, and never by mistaking a shared
image for shared agreement.
