# Framework — the layered combined map

_Owned by the `synthesizer` agent. Seed version (Stage 2)._

The framework is a **stack of layers**, from what is established to what is symbolic. Each element names its
tier and confidence. Nothing in a lower layer is allowed to borrow the standing of a higher one; the
cross-domain links between layers are the labeled **bridges**, not promotions.

## Layer 1 — Established core (E1)

The load-bearing, proven/confirmed material.

- **Symmetry ⇒ conservation** — Noether's theorem (`noether-symmetry-conservation`, E1, derived). A genuine
  theorem; the honest meaning of "symmetry" everything else gestures at.
- **Decoherence** (`qm-decoherence`, E1) — explains the *appearance* of collapse; does **not** settle the
  measurement problem.
- **The five Platonic solids** (`platonic-solids-five`, E1) and **E8** (`e8-lie-group`, E1) — the real
  mathematics later dressed as "sacred geometry" or "theory of everything." Beautiful, and only mathematics.

These two — Noether and E8 — are linked by genuine **shared-mathematics** (Lie groups):
`symmetry-shared-mathematics` (ceiling E1). This is the model of an honest bridge.

## Layer 2 — Speculative extensions (E2–E3)

Serious but unconfirmed, or contested.

- **Many-worlds** (`many-worlds-interpretation`, E2) — takes unitary QM literally; not experimentally
  distinguished from rivals.
- **Lisi's E8 TOE** (`lisi-e8-toe`, E3, disputed) — a falsifiable physics use of E8, largely refuted by a
  published no-go result. The cautionary twin of the E1 E8 mathematics.
- **IIT** (`iit-integrated-information`, E3) and **Orch-OR** (`orch-or-microtubules`, E3) — the two contested
  attempts to physically ground experience. COGITATE (2025) confirmed neither IIT's nor GNWT's key
  predictions; Orch-OR still faces Tegmark's decoherence objection. The 2025 anesthesia work is suggestive,
  not confirmatory.
- **LLM self-reports** (`llm-subjective-reports`, E3) — a real behavior; the machine-sentience reading is a
  *separate*, weaker claim.

## Layer 3 — Philosophical interpretation (E4)

Where O Theory actually lives as a *theory*: it is an interpretive response to the hard problem.

- **The hard problem** (`hard-problem-of-consciousness`, E4) — the argument that structures the field.
- **Cosmopsychism** (`cosmopsychism`, E4) — the academic cousin of the "O-Source": consciousness as
  fundamental, individual minds as derivative. A live position with a real objection (decombination).

The framework's spine is the **speculation** that quantum observer-puzzles and the hard problem share one
resolution — consciousness/information as substrate (`consciousness-as-substrate-speculation`, ceiling E4).
Labeled speculation, listed among the open problems, never asserted.

## Layer 4 — Symbolic / contemplative resonance (E5)

Included on its own terms, never as physics.

- **Advaita non-duality** (`advaita-nonduality`, E5) — Ātman = Brahman, as the tradition's claim and
  practice. Its mapping onto O Theory ("Brahman = O-Source") is **metaphor**, gathered with many-worlds and
  cosmopsychism under `one-field-metaphor` (ceiling E5).
- **Sacred geometry** — the Platonic solids and E8 as symbols. The mathematics is E1; the symbolic reading
  is E5; the claim that φ is a universal law of beauty is **E6** (`golden-ratio-ubiquity`, rejected) and is
  kept out of the support structure entirely.

## The spine, in one sentence

A proven core of symmetry and mathematics (E1); a contested middle (E2–E3) — serious-but-unconfirmed
physics (many-worlds) alongside contested attempts to physically ground experience (IIT, Orch-OR); a
philosophical proposal that consciousness is fundamental (E4); and a symbolic tradition that has reached
for the same *image* for millennia (E5) — connected by four honestly-labeled bridges, not by a claim that
any layer proves the next, and not by mistaking the shared image for shared agreement.
