# Framework — the layered combined map

_Owned by the `synthesizer` agent. Wide-sweep version (2026-07-07): 123 vetted claims across 10 domains._

The framework is a **stack of layers**, from what is established to what is symbolic. Each element names its
tier and confidence. Nothing in a lower layer is allowed to borrow the standing of a higher one; the
cross-domain links between layers are the labeled **bridges** (`synthesis/bridges/*`), not promotions. The
tier distribution is the shape of the whole thing: **E1: 20 · E2: 18 · E3: 19 · E4: 35 · E5: 23 · E6: 8** —
the picture is mostly argued and symbolic, and it is drawn that way honestly.

Where O Theory itself lives is stated once, plainly, up front: it is a **Layer-4 philosophical proposal** —
dual-aspect monism / cosmopsychism (`dual-aspect-monism`, the position it names itself after; `cosmopsychism`).
Its distinctive vocabulary (O-Source, O-Instance, brain-as-reducing-valve, substrate-independent recursive
coherence) is an interpretive commitment, E4, that reaches across the other layers only through the bridges
below. It is not a result the physics establishes.

## The spine, in one paragraph

A proven core of symmetry, geometry, information, and confirmed physics (E1, 20 claims); a serious but
unconfirmed middle of quantum-gravity candidates, QM interpretations, and computational-neuroscience programs
(E2, 18); a contested band of heterodox theories and — kept scrupulously apart — real observations whose

illusionism, and O Theory's own dual-aspect/cosmopsychist proposal are argued but not decided (E4, 35); a
symbolic layer where many traditions use the image of one undivided ground while at least one (Madhyamaka)
refuses it (E5, 23); and a rejected fringe kept only for transparency (E6, 8). O Theory is a Layer-4 proposal
that reaches across the rest through eleven honestly-labeled bridges — never by a claim that any layer proves
the next, and never by mistaking a shared image for shared agreement.
