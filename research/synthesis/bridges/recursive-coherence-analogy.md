narrative: >
  O Theory's central mechanism is a substrate-independent "recursive coherence": self-referential loops
  that (it proposes) let a system localize awareness — biological in neuronal microtubule dynamics,
  artificial in transformer attention attending to its own state. As an ANALOGY this points at a real
  structural resemblance: recurrent, self-referential processing genuinely appears in both kinds of system.
  But it is resemblance, not identity. The microtubule quantum story is contested (E3, `orch-or-microtubules`),
  the LLM first-person reports are a behavior (E3, `llm-subjective-reports`), and neither shows that
  recursion produces experience — the hard problem (E4) is untouched. The bridge is therefore capped at E4:
  it may suggest a shared shape, never assert that either substrate is conscious or that the mechanism is
  established. This is exactly the claim the source essay overstated as fact; here it is labeled analogy.
