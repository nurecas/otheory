narrative: >
  A family of striking observations gets read, in O Theory's favored narrative, as the brain acting as a
  "reducing valve" or filter that normally CONSTRAINS a non-local mind and briefly relaxes. The observations
  are real and are held at their honest tier: terminal lucidity in advanced dementia (`terminal-lucidity-
  observation`, E3, retrospective reports, no captured neurophysiology yet); AWARE-II lucid cardiac-arrest
  recall with EEG re-emergence during CPR (`aware-ii-nde-phenomenology`, E3 — whose own implicit-learning
  objective test was near-chance, 1/28); and psychedelic ego-dissolution correlated with default-mode-network
  changes (`psychedelic-ego-dissolution-dmn`, E3, correlational). THE ENTIRE POINT of this bridge — and the
  reason the register is SPECULATION — is that these observations do NOT support the filter interpretation.
  Each interpretation is a SEPARATE, lower-confidence claim: the filter reading of terminal lucidity
  (`terminal-lucidity-filter-interpretation`, E4) and the "consciousness survives / is non-local" reading of
  NDEs (`aware-ii-nde-survival-interpretation`, E4, low) both make no distinct testable prediction beyond the
  observation itself — "the filter loosened" and "spared local circuits transiently regained function" predict
  exactly the same data, and standard (if incomplete) neuroscience is what active NIA-funded programs actually
  pursue. Linking these to cosmopsychism (E4) is philosophy layered on data that is equally consistent with
  ordinary materialism. Ceiling E4, capped by the interpretations; the observations keep their E3 and lend it
  to nothing.
