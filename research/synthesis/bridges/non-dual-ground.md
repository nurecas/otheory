narrative: >
  Across many traditions a single, undivided, ineffable ground recurs: Advaita's Brahman (E5), Plotinus' the
  One (E5), the Dao (E5), Kabbalah's Ein Sof (E5), Ibn Arabi's waḥdat al-wujūd (E5). O Theory reads its
  "O-Source" as a modern cousin of these, alongside the E4 philosophical positions that make consciousness
  fundamental — cosmopsychism (E4) and analytic idealism (`analytic-idealism-kastrup`, E4). As METAPHOR the
  resonance is real and is why the Portrait reaches for "white light through a prism." But this bridge exists
  mainly to state its OWN honest limits, and they are large. First: the traditions do NOT all agree.
  Madhyamaka's śūnyatā (`madhyamaka-sunyata-emptiness`, E5) explicitly REFUSES reification into any "ground of
  being" or "One" — the tradition declares emptiness itself empty precisely to block that move — so folding it
  in with Brahman and Ein Sof misrepresents it. Second: "they all point at the same reality" is the perennial-
  philosophy thesis (`perennial-philosophy-common-core-thesis`), which is a CONTESTED E4 comparative claim, not
  a fact — Katz's constructivism holds the experiences are shaped differently all the way down, and we have
  only already-interpreted reports to compare. Third: even the E4 metaphysics (cosmopsychism, idealism) is
  argued, not shown, and faces the decombination problem. So the ceiling is E5, the register is metaphor, and
  the narrative's job is to forbid two launderings at once: symbol restated as physics, and apparent
  convergence restated as agreement.
