narrative: >
  Across many traditions a single, undivided, ineffable ground recurs: Advaita's Brahman (E5), Plotinus' the
  One (E5), the Dao (E5), Kabbalah's Ein Sof (E5), Ibn Arabi's waḥdat al-wujūd (E5). O Theory reads its
  "O-Source" as a modern cousin of these, alongside the E4 philosophical positions that make consciousness
  fundamental — cosmopsychism (E4) and analytic idealism (`analytic-idealism-kastrup`, E4). The experience-
  first metaphysics of the Nameless project (nurecas.com) (`nameless-experience-first-metaphysics`, E4) is a
  further contemporary member of this family: an idealist/cosmopsychist monism that explicitly invokes
  Advaita's non-duality and Nāgārjuna's two truths, and that — creditably — marks its own alignment of
  śūnyatā with a physical "still point" as "structural rather than derived... a convergence, not a
  translation," not a shared mathematics. As METAPHOR the resonance across all of these is real and is why
  the Portrait reaches for "white light through a prism." But this bridge exists mainly to state its OWN
  honest limits, and they are large. First: the traditions do NOT all agree. Madhyamaka's śūnyatā
  (`madhyamaka-sunyata-emptiness`, E5) explicitly REFUSES reification into any "ground of being" or "One" —
  the tradition declares emptiness itself empty precisely to block that move — so folding it in with Brahman
  and Ein Sof (or with any experiential ground) misrepresents it. Second: "they all point at the same
  reality" is the perennial-philosophy thesis (`perennial-philosophy-common-core-thesis`), which is a
  CONTESTED E4 comparative claim, not a fact — Katz's constructivism holds the experiences are shaped
  differently all the way down, and we have only already-interpreted reports to compare. Third: even the E4
  metaphysics (cosmopsychism, idealism, the Nameless experience-first view) is argued, not shown, and each
  faces the decombination problem — how one ground yields many bounded private subjects. So the ceiling is
  E5, the register is metaphor, and the narrative's job is to forbid two launderings at once: symbol restated
  as physics, and apparent convergence restated as agreement.
