narrative: >
  "Observer" is the single most laundered word in this whole field, and the point of this bridge is to hold
  its senses apart, not to fuse them. In physics the "observer" is a measurement interaction: decoherence
  (`qm-decoherence`, E1) shows that entanglement with an environment — a detector, a photon, air molecules —
  suppresses interference with NO mind required, and the measurement problem (`measurement-problem-statement`,
  E1) is a logical tension in the formalism, not a puzzle about awareness. Relational QM (E2) makes facts
  relative to any interacting physical system, and its own SEP source states it excludes mentalistic content.
  Against all of that stands the one claim in the corpus that literally identifies the observer with a mind:
  von Neumann–Wigner consciousness-collapse (`von-neumann-wigner-consciousness-collapse`, E3, disputed). THE
  HONEST LIMIT, and the whole reason the ceiling is E3: the physics observer is NOT a mind. Decoherence
  independently dissolves the original motivation for pushing the "cut" as far as consciousness, the quantum
  Zeno effect already falsifies the naive versions (Chalmers & McQueen 2022), and de Barros & Oas (2017) show
  the surviving versions are near-unfalsifiable. The link between "observation matters in QM" and "mind
  collapses the wavefunction" is therefore ANALOGY at best — a shared word, not a shared referent — capped at
  the E3 standing of its single supporting claim, and flagged in open-problems as the load-bearing weak point
  of any QM→consciousness bridge O Theory reaches for.
