narrative: >
  A recognizable family of recent theory-of-everything proposals shares one ambition: to grow all of
  physics — the Standard Model gauge group, the constants of nature, sometimes gravity — from a single
  geometric or informational seed. Nielsen's Topological Unified Field Theory
  (`nielsen-topological-unified-field-theory`, E3) takes the seed to be the complex Hopf fibration
  S^1 → S^9 → CP^4, reading the classification of U(1) bundles as forcing a "canonical space." The Nameless
  project (nurecas.com) (`nameless-qubit-standard-model-derivation`, E3) takes the seed to be the geometry of
  a single qubit — the Bloch sphere — and counts its eigenmodes to reach SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1), the GUT weak-mixing
  value sin^2θ_W = 3/8, the Koide relation, and a sharp neutrino prediction. Lisi's E8 model
  (`lisi-e8-toe`, E3) took the seed to be a single exceptional Lie group. All three sit in the same
  "it-from-geometry / it-from-qubit" current as Wheeler's slogan (`it-from-bit-information-ontology`, E4) and
  the genuine informational reconstruction of quantum theory (`wheeler-it-from-bit-information-physics`, E2).
  This bridge is labeled SPECULATION and is capped at E3 — the highest tier any of its physics-claim members
  reaches — and it may go no higher. It must never borrow the E1 standing of the mathematics these programs
  invoke (that is the separate `hopf-fibration-shared-mathematics` bridge, and the shared mathematics does
  NOT license the unification conclusion). The load-bearing honesty here is that "a simple geometric or
  informational seed grounds all of physics" is a hope with two recurring hazards: (1) small-integer or
  golden-ratio matches to already-measured constants (sin^2θ_W = 3/8 is the known SU(5) value; 4/13 ≈ 0.3077;
  golden-ratio fits to the fine-structure constant) risk numerology — there are many simple expressions near
  any target, and a fit is not a mechanism; and (2) these works are largely self-published (viXra, Zenodo,
  GitHub, a young non-Scopus journal) and not independently reviewed. That is why the same current also
  contains outright-rejected members filed elsewhere at E6 (`ihc-inverted-hypersphere-cosmology`,
  `observer-patch-holography-toe`, `haramein-schwarzschild-proton-holographic-mass`) — the boundary between
  E3 and E6 here is drawn by author credentials and venue, not by confirmation, and none of them has been
  confirmed. One member makes a genuinely near-term falsifiable claim: the Nameless prediction
  sin^2θ_12 = 4/13, which JUNO (~2030) can decide. Until an independent check or JUNO reports, the whole
  family stays exactly here — an intriguing, honestly-labeled speculation, not a result.
