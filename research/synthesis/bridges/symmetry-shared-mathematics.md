narrative: >
  This is one of the connections in the corpus that genuinely earns the label "shared-mathematics."
  Noether's theorem and E8 are not analogous — they are the same mathematics: the theory of continuous
  (Lie) symmetry groups. Noether ties every differentiable symmetry of an action to a conserved current;
  E8 is the largest exceptional simple Lie group, a maximal object in exactly that theory of symmetry.
  Because both sides are established mathematics, the connection carries an E1 ceiling. Its companion is
  `hopf-fibration-shared-mathematics`, the other genuinely rigorous shared-mathematics bridge (the Hopf
  fibration and U(1) bundle classification), which is likewise E1 for the mathematics and likewise fences
  off the unification conclusions drawn from it. Crucially, this does NOT extend to symbolic or mystical
  uses of the word "symmetry": the moment "symmetry" is stretched to metaphysical unity, the register
  changes to analogy or metaphor and the ceiling drops (see `one-field-metaphor`). Shown here to make the
  honest case that some cross-domain links are real and rigorous — which is what makes the fenced ones
  trustworthy.
