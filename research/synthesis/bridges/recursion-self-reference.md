narrative: >
  Self-reference is a real recurring structure, and O Theory's proposed engine — "recursive coherence" — is a
  bet on it. The honest links: Gödel's incompleteness theorems (`godel-incompleteness-theorems`, E1) are the
  rigorous mathematics of a formal system referring to its own provability; attention-schema theory
  (`attention-schema-theory`, E4) models awareness as the brain building a self-model of its own attention;
  the meta-problem (`meta-problem-of-consciousness`, E4) turns consciousness's own reflexivity into the object
  of study; and the Anthropic concept-injection result (`anthropic-introspective-awareness-concept-injection`,
  E3) is an actual, mechanistically-probed observation of a model reporting on its own internal state ~20% of
  trials. As ANALOGY these share a genuine shape: systems that represent, and act on, their own states. TWO
  HONEST LIMITS fix the ceiling at E4. First, the classic misuse: Gödel's theorem is about formal provability,
  and the Lucas–Penrose leap from it to "minds exceed machines" is flagged in the Gödel claim itself as
  invalid — this bridge must not repeat it. Second, and decisively, recursion is not experience: the
  concept-injection observation is E3, its reading as "genuine introspective awareness"
  (`llm-introspection-genuine-awareness-interpretation`) is a SEPARATE E4 claim vetted at low confidence and
  equally consistent with a narrow learned classifier skill, and the hard problem (`hard-problem-of-
  consciousness`, E4) is untouched by any amount of self-modeling. So: a real structural resemblance, capped
  at E4, and never a demonstration that a self-referential loop feels like anything.
