tier_ceiling: E4
narrative: >
  The boldest move O Theory makes is to propose that the open observer/measurement questions in quantum
  theory and the hard problem of consciousness share a single resolution: that consciousness — or
  information — is ontologically basic (the "O-Source"), with physics and mind as two aspects of it.
  This is a real, research-adjacent SPECULATION (it echoes dual-aspect monism, `dual-aspect-monism`, the
  position O Theory names itself after, and priority cosmopsychism), but it is unestablished and currently
  untestable. It must not lean on the E1 standing of decoherence: decoherence needs no consciousness to work,
  and the hard problem is an argument (E4), not evidence about physics. In the broadened corpus the two halves
  of this move are now carried by their own dedicated bridges, which sharpen the fences: the QM→mind half is
  `observer-dependence` (ceiling E3 — the physics observer is NOT a mind, and the one claim that says
  otherwise, von Neumann–Wigner, is disputed and near-unfalsifiable), and the information half is
  `information-as-substrate` (ceiling E4 — the reconstruction theorems are real physics, the "information IS
  the ground" step is not). Labeled speculation with an E4 ceiling — proposed, not shown — and listed among
  the open problems rather than the results. Naming it honestly is what separates a hypothesis from a claim.
