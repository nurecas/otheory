tier_ceiling: E4
narrative: >
  The boldest move O Theory makes is to propose that the open observer/measurement questions in quantum
  theory and the hard problem of consciousness share a single resolution: that consciousness — or
  information — is ontologically basic (the "O-Source"), with physics and mind as two aspects of it.
  This is a real, research-adjacent SPECULATION (it echoes dual-aspect monism and priority cosmopsychism),
  but it is unestablished and currently untestable. It must not lean on the E1 standing of decoherence:
  decoherence needs no consciousness to work, and the hard problem is an argument (E4), not evidence about
  physics. Labeled speculation with an E4 ceiling — proposed, not shown — and listed among the open
  problems rather than the results. Naming it honestly is what separates a hypothesis from a claim.
