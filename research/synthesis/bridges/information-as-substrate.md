narrative: >
  Information genuinely recurs as a technical object across three established or seriously-developed results,
  and this part of the bridge is real SHARED-MATHEMATICS. Shannon (E1) proved the coding theorems that make
  "information" a precise, measurable quantity. Chiribella–D'Ariano–Perinotti's informational reconstruction
  of quantum theory (`wheeler-it-from-bit-information-physics`, E2) derives the QM formalism from six
  information-theoretic axioms. The holographic principle / AdS-CFT (`holographic-principle-ads-cft`, E2)
  ties a region's degrees of freedom to bits on its boundary (the Bekenstein-Hawking area law counts entropy
  in bits, `black-hole-entropy-area-law`, E1). These share literal mathematics — entropy, distinguishability,
  bit-counting — not merely a mood. THE HONEST LIMIT: the register drops the instant the story crosses from
  "information is the right mathematics" to "information IS the ground of being." That ontological step —
  Wheeler's slogan (`it-from-bit-information-ontology`, E4), Tegmark's MUH (E4), pancomputationalism
  (`pancomputationalism-digital-physics`, E4) — is speculation, and each carries a fatal-or-open objection in
  its own file (the SEP notes "little positive evidence" for the ontic reading; the description-vs-thing-
  described conflation is unresolved). So the ceiling is E4, and it is E4 by the philosophy, not the physics:
  the reconstruction theorem is a statement about QM's axiomatic economy, not a discovery that our universe is
  made of bits. O Theory's "information as substrate" language may lean on the E1/E2 results as motivation but
  MUST NOT inherit their standing for the E4 metaphysics built on top.
