  The image of a single, undivided ground recurs across very different registers: Everett's one universal
  wavefunction, Advaita's non-dual Brahman, cosmopsychism's single cosmic subject. The resonance is
  genuine as IMAGERY — it is why O Theory reaches for the "white light through a prism" picture — but the
  resemblance is metaphorical, not structural. A wavefunction is not a subject; "oneness" means something
  different in unitary quantum dynamics (E2), in a metaphysical position (E4), and in a contemplative
  soteriology (E5). Labeled metaphor with an E5 ceiling so that the physics is never read as evidence for
  the metaphysics or the mysticism, and vice versa. Evocative, and allowed to be — in Zone B, clearly
  marked.
