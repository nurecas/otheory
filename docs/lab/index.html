McKay's bridge — one group Γ ⊂ SU(2), two faces

The affine ADE ↔ binary-polyhedral dictionary: Ê₆ ↔ 2T, Ê₇ ↔ 2O, Ê₈ ↔ 2I. The exceptional root lattice and the Hopf fibration are the same finite subgroup Γ ⊂ S³ seen two ways: as a lattice (its elements + nearest-neighbour graph) and as a Hopf fibration (left cosets of a cyclic S¹ ⊂ Γ — genuine great-circle fibres).

The E8 mandala is a Hopf fibration

The 240 roots of E8 you know from the Coxeter plane (the 30-fold mandala) and the quaternionic Hopf fibration S⁷ → S⁴ are the same 240 points. One chirality of that fibration sorts them into 10 fibres of exactly 24 — a 24-cell on each vertex of the 5-orthoplex base. 10 × 24 = 240 (verified). This is settled mathematics — Sadoc & Mosseri's route from E8 to real icosahedral quasicrystals. Drag unfold to watch the mandala open into its fibres; colour = fibre.

The geometry above is exact, settled mathematics (E8 ↔ Hopf ↔ 24-cells ↔ quasicrystals — real matter). Reading it as the root configuration of the universe is a separate, contested claim: Lisi's E8 theory is the serious version, but Distler–Garibaldi proved E8 cannot hold all three fermion generations without a mirror antigeneration of the wrong chirality — and that obstruction is chirality itself, the very structure drawn here. Chirality is conjugation — h_R = a · h_L · a⁻¹

Same ℝ⁸ = ℍ⊕ℍ, same partition — but here is why it isn't numerology. For every root with a ≠ 0 (all 216, verified exactly), the two chiralities h_R = bā and h_L = āb are conjugate quaternions. Three things fall out: they share a real part (one 10-point base, not two); conjugation fixes the centre of ℍ, so h_L = h_R exactly on the real values — the 4 diagonal 24-cells are the centre; and the six imaginary units are a single conjugacy class, so the 6×6 grid is complete — no cell can be empty.

Not a subsystem split — the four 24-cells are D₄'s; their union isn't a rank-8 subsystem.  Canonical only up to W(E8) — the 6×6 depends on the quaternionic structure (all conjugate); structure invariant, labels not.  No group lift — W(H₄)×W(H₄) ⊄ W(E8) (5⁴ vs 5²); incidence geometry, not a chiral Weyl factorization.

The ℍ² chiral node lattice — E8-native

Fix ℝ⁸ = ℍ ⊕ ℍ (a D₄⊕D₄ frame). The two Hopf maps on S⁷, h_R = (|a|²−|b|², 2bā) and h_L = (|a|²−|b|², 2āb), send all 240 roots onto the same 10 points of S⁴ — the 5-orthoplex {0, ∞} ∪ Q₈. The joint map (h_L, h_R) then partitions the roots. No folding, no φ-scaling, no rank drop.

It is a partition, not a subsystem split — 96 and 144 aren't rank-8 root-subsystem sizes; don't read them as Lie subalgebras.  Canonical only up to W(E8) — the labelling depends on the D₄⊕D₄ choice (all conjugate); the structure is invariant, the labels aren't.  No group lift — W(H₄)×W(H₄) ⊄ W(E8); this is geometry, not a chiral Weyl factorization.
