A synthesis of the theories of everything

O Theory

A synthesis of humanity's theories of what reality is — built into a single honest proposal for what it all amounts to.

O Theory is a synthesis. It gathers humanity's serious attempts to explain what reality fundamentally is — physics, mathematics, the study of the mind, philosophy, and the contemplative traditions — and weaves them into a single proposed theory of everything: one account, built from the parts, of what they might together amount to. What follows, just below, is that theory.

It is built in the open. The synthesis is candid about how much weight each source was given — from the proven core it may never contradict, through the frontier theories it treats as leads, to the traditions it hears as testimony — and about where the joins between the fields genuinely hold. It is offered as a proposal, not a proof. The honest apparatus beneath it, and the map of every idea it draws on, follow in the section after.

The synthesis

One Reality, Two Faces: A Proposed Theory of Everything

O Theory proposes that reality is, at bottom, a single thing — but not a single stuff. Not a lump of matter, not a cloud of particles, not even a field in the ordinary sense. The one reality it proposes is a process: something that goes on, that carries and arranges information, that loops back to observe and model itself, and that has, woven through it, an inner felt aspect. Everything else — galaxies, atoms, laws, brains, the sensation of reading this sentence — is proposed to be that one process seen from some angle, at some scale, folded in some way. The theory's whole claim can be compressed into a single line and then unpacked: reality is one self-referential, information-bearing process with two faces, an outer face that physics measures and an inner face that is experience, and it appears as many separate minds wherever it folds densely enough to observe itself locally.

Start with the outer face, because it is the one that can be checked. When physicists describe the world, the deepest and most reliable things they find are not objects but invariances — rules about what can be changed without changing anything that matters. Move an experiment to the other side of the room, run it tomorrow instead of today, turn the apparatus to face a different wall: the laws come out the same. And there is a proven mathematical fact, over a century old, that every such symmetry is bound to a quantity that never changes — energy, momentum, electric charge. Symmetry and conservation are two descriptions of one situation. This is the outer face's grammar. The world is not a heap of independent facts; it is a structure held together by what stays the same under change. To this, physics adds two more deep regularities: that information in any system can be counted, and that this count sets hard limits — even on how much a region of space, or a black hole, can contain; and that the same forms and the same rules recur across wildly different scales, from the very small to the very large, in ways that can be tracked precisely. Invariance, information, recurrence across scale — these are not decorations on reality. In O Theory they are read as the grammar of the one process: the lawful, repeating, countable regularities that any single self-structuring thing would display when looked at from outside.

Now the inner face, which is the one the outer description keeps failing to reach. Give the most complete physical account imaginable of a human brain — every cell, every signal, every electrochemical event, every computation it performs — and something is still, stubbornly, left out: the plain fact that being that brain feels like something from the inside. There is redness in seeing red, ache in an ache, a felt presence to being awake at all, and no list of physical mechanisms, however exhaustive, seems to contain it or entail it. This is the hard problem of consciousness, and O Theory takes its persistence seriously rather than treating it as a temporary gap. The theory's reading is this: experience is not squeezed out of the outer description because it was never a product of the outer description in the first place. The felt inner glow and the measurable outer structure are not one manufactured from the other. They are two faces of one reality — the same single process, described from within and described from without. This is the position philosophers call dual-aspect monism, and it is O Theory's committed interpretive move: mind and matter are not two substances, and neither is an illusion produced by the other; they are the inside and the outside of one thing.

What binds the two faces together is a feature the process has that inert stuff does not: it is self-referential. It loops back on itself. It contains descriptions of itself, models of itself, observations of itself. This is not a mystical flourish; it is exactly where the strangeness in our most precise theories lives. In quantum physics, the act of observation or measurement occupies a role that a century of brilliant work has not been able to make ordinary — the world seems to wait for a measurement to settle what is the case, and physicists still argue about what a measurement even is. In mathematics, any system rich enough to talk about itself runs headlong into exact, unavoidable limits, first proven for arithmetic: self-reference carves permanent holes in what a system can prove about itself. O Theory reads both as the same signature. A reality that models and observes itself will show, in its outer face, precisely this: a physics where observation is not a passive bystander, and a mathematics that cannot fully close the loop on itself. Self-reference is the hinge on which the inner and outer faces turn into each other — for to observe is already to have an inside.

That gives the theory its account of us. If reality is one self-observing process with an inner felt aspect, then a mind is not a separate lamp switched on inside a dark universe. A mind is a place where the one process folds back on itself densely — a brain being exactly such a knot of feedback, self-modeling, and recursive coherence. And O Theory proposes that at such a knot the single awareness that runs through the whole is not created but narrowed: focused, bounded, and sealed into a private point of view. The image is a prism, or the older image of a reducing valve. A prism does not manufacture light; it selects and bends what is already there. A valve does not create the pressure; it narrows the flow. On this picture the brain does not generate consciousness so much as focus it — concentrating the one wide awareness into the vivid, walled-off, first-person perspective of a single person, which is why each of us seems to be a separate island of experience that cannot read any other mind. The separateness is real as a focusing, not as a final fact.

And here the oldest testimony in the human record lines up with the newest philosophy. For thousands of years, practitioners in cultures with no contact with one another have reported, after long stillness, the same strange thing: that beneath the busy separate self there is a single, undivided ground of awareness, and that the sense of being a sealed-off individual can soften and open onto it. O Theory reads these reports not as measurements of the world but as first-person corroboration — a description, from the inside, of what it is like when the local focusing relaxes and the narrowed beam widens back toward the whole. It is exactly the inner-face report one would expect if the theory were true.

So the pieces lock together. The deep themes that keep recurring across every field of human inquiry — symmetry, information, self-reference, observation, scale — are not five unrelated curiosities. In O Theory they are read as the signatures of a single self-aware, self- structuring reality, glimpsed from different angles. Symmetry and scale are the grammar of its outer face. Information is the medium in which a self-referential process must work. Observation and self-reference are where the loop of self-modeling shows through into physics and logic. The hard problem is the fingerprint of its inner face. And the theory of everything, on this proposal, is not a final equation but a single statement about what all the equations are about: one self-referential, experiential, information-structured reality, whose outer face is physics and whose inner face is mind, appearing as many localized minds wherever it folds back densely enough to watch itself.

02 · Method & Map

How the synthesis was made

The synthesis is not written by hand and it is not an opinion. It is drawn from a knowledge base built by a disciplined process. Each field is surveyed for its serious theories. Every theory becomes a single, self-contained claim with at least one real, checkable source. An independent reviewer then re-checks that source and assigns the claim a tier — a plain statement of how much the claim has actually earned. A final reviewer tries to break the result, catching any place where a weaker idea has been dressed up as a stronger one. Only then does the claim enter the map.

The tiers are the heart of the method. They are the reason a proven theorem and a contemplative report can sit in the same synthesis without being confused for one another.

How the theory was assembled, and what it rests on

A theory built from such varied materials is only honest if it says how much weight each kind of source was given, and marks the strength of every joint. The rule was simple and strict.

The proven core of physics and mathematics — symmetry bound to conservation, the counting and limiting of information, the exact limits of self-reference, the lawful recurrence across scale — was given the highest standing, but specifically as constraint. It is the shape the finished theory must fit inside and is never permitted to overrule. Nothing from philosophy or contemplation may contradict it. The unconfirmed frontier — the rival theories of quantum measurement, of gravity and spacetime, of what a conscious brain is doing — was treated as suggestive leads only, held loosely, because it is striking how often these frontiers reach for information, geometry, and observation, but none of it is settled. Philosophy supplied the committed interpretive move — that experience is fundamental, one face of the one reality — and this is load-bearing but explicitly an argued position, not a discovered fact. The contemplative traditions were heard as symbolic corroboration, first-person testimony, never as data about the physical world. And the fringe — universal-beauty numerology, cosmic-blueprint claims, astrology, morphic resonance, physics-costumed models that fail on their own numbers — was given zero weight, kept in the record only to show it was checked and set aside.

The theory is built on four seams, each labeled for exactly how strong it is. Symmetry is the strongest: it is not a resemblance but literally the same mathematics — the language of groups — running through the physics of forces and the geometry of shapes. Information is next: here the mathematics is genuinely shared across coding theory, black-hole accounting, and a real reconstruction of quantum theory, but the further step, that information is what reality is made of, is speculation, not a theorem, and is marked as such. The observer is weaker: the strange place of observation in quantum theory is established, but the claim that the observer must be a conscious mind is only an analogy, and it carries a load-bearing objection — the one account in the corpus that literally makes a mind collapse the wavefunction is disputed and undercut by known physics. The one ground is the softest seam of all — an E5 metaphor, a shared image across traditions, and the least binding, since only already- interpreted reports can be compared and at least one great tradition refuses the very idea of a ground.

Said plainly, then: this is a proposal. In the vocabulary this project holds itself to, it is an argued philosophical construction — the kind of claim decided by reason, not by measurement (an E4 position) — and it is offered as exactly that, never laundered into confirmed physics, always constrained by the proven core. Recent independent efforts argue for closely related positions on their own terms, so the proposal does not stand alone; but company is not confirmation. Its strongest objections are named rather than hidden. First, the decombination problem: if awareness is one undivided ground, why does it appear as billions of separate, sealed selves who cannot read one another's minds? No accepted solution exists — the prism image is a picture, not yet a mechanism. Second, the traditions do not truly agree: the near- universal "one ground" is itself a contested reading, and one major tradition deliberately refuses any ground at all, calling even emptiness empty precisely to block the move. Third, illusionism, the rival that would dissolve the whole edifice: the view that there is no inner felt glow to explain in the first place, only a very convincing internal report of one. If that is right, the question this theory is built to answer never needed answering.

These are not reasons to withhold the theory. They are the reasons it is offered as a proposal and defended as one — one picture of the whole, deliberately built, weighted openly, joined at its real seams, and put forward to be judged.

The Hopf fibration as literal shared mathematics — the same bundle in the qubit, the monopole, and U(1) bundle classification

This is a second genuine "shared-mathematics" bridge, alongside symmetry-shared-mathematics. The Hopf fibration S^1 ↪ S^3 → S^2 (hopf-fibration-topology-physics, E1) is not a metaphor that turns up in several places — it is literally the same fiber bundle recurring across confirmed physics. The base S^2 is the Bloch sphere of a two-level quantum system, so the fibration is exactly the state-space topology of a qubit; the total-space-over-base structure is exactly the fiber bundle of the Dirac magnetic monopole, which is why electric charge is quantized; and Urbantke's peer-reviewed survey catalogues at least seven such independent appearances (Bloch sphere, harmonic oscillator, Taub-NUT spacetime, twistors, Wigner helicity, monopole, Dirac equation). The complex circle bundles S^1 ↪ S^{2n+1} → CP^n extend to the universal U(1)-bundle over CP^∞ = BU(1) = K(Z,2), so that every U(1) bundle over any paracompact space is classified by H^2(X;Z) (u1-bundles-classified-by-cp-infinity, E1) — the topological reason U(1) flux and charge are quantized. All of this is settled algebraic topology and standard theoretical physics; it shares the same subject matter as group theory (group-theory-symmetry-mathematics, E1) and the Lie-group machinery behind the Standard Model (standard-model-gauge-theory, E1) and E8 (e8-lie-group, E1). Hence the E1 ceiling. THE HONEST LIMIT, stated once and enforced: that a basic low-dimensional nontrivial bundle recurs wherever U(1) phases, two-state systems, or normed division algebras appear carries NO automatic ontological weight. The inference from "the Hopf fibration appears seven times" to "the Hopf fibration is THE canonical space of nature" is a separate, unlicensed step — it belongs to nielsen-topological-unified-field-theory (E3) and to the geometry-or-information-as-ground speculation bridge, and it may never borrow the E1 standing of the mathematics recorded here. The classifying-space theorem constrains abelian U(1) bundles only; it does not single out any finite Hopf stage (such as S^1 → S^9 → CP^4) as physically privileged, nor classify the nonabelian SU(2)/SU(3) bundles of the Standard Model. The shared mathematics is real; the unification conclusion drawn from it is not, and this bridge does not carry it.

Symmetry as literal shared mathematics — Noether's theorem and E8

This is one of the connections in the corpus that genuinely earns the label "shared-mathematics." Noether's theorem and E8 are not analogous — they are the same mathematics: the theory of continuous (Lie) symmetry groups. Noether ties every differentiable symmetry of an action to a conserved current; E8 is the largest exceptional simple Lie group, a maximal object in exactly that theory of symmetry. Because both sides are established mathematics, the connection carries an E1 ceiling. Its companion is hopf-fibration-shared-mathematics, the other genuinely rigorous shared-mathematics bridge (the Hopf fibration and U(1) bundle classification), which is likewise E1 for the mathematics and likewise fences off the unification conclusions drawn from it. Crucially, this does NOT extend to symbolic or mystical uses of the word "symmetry": the moment "symmetry" is stretched to metaphysical unity, the register changes to analogy or metaphor and the ceiling drops (see one-field-metaphor). Shown here to make the honest case that some cross-domain links are real and rigorous — which is what makes the fenced ones trustworthy.

The three "observers" — measurement/decoherence, relational facts, and the mind that allegedly collapses the wavefunction

"Observer" is the single most laundered word in this whole field, and the point of this bridge is to hold its senses apart, not to fuse them. In physics the "observer" is a measurement interaction: decoherence (qm-decoherence, E1) shows that entanglement with an environment — a detector, a photon, air molecules — suppresses interference with NO mind required, and the measurement problem (measurement-problem-statement, E1) is a logical tension in the formalism, not a puzzle about awareness. Relational QM (E2) makes facts relative to any interacting physical system, and its own SEP source states it excludes mentalistic content. Against all of that stands the one claim in the corpus that literally identifies the observer with a mind: von Neumann–Wigner consciousness-collapse (von-neumann-wigner-consciousness-collapse, E3, disputed). THE HONEST LIMIT, and the whole reason the ceiling is E3: the physics observer is NOT a mind. Decoherence independently dissolves the original motivation for pushing the "cut" as far as consciousness, the quantum Zeno effect already falsifies the naive versions (Chalmers & McQueen 2022), and de Barros & Oas (2017) show the surviving versions are near-unfalsifiable. The link between "observation matters in QM" and "mind collapses the wavefunction" is therefore ANALOGY at best — a shared word, not a shared referent — capped at the E3 standing of its single supporting claim, and flagged in open-problems as the load-bearing weak point of any QM→consciousness bridge O Theory reaches for.

05 · Open Questions

Where the map runs out

And this is where honesty demands we stop — the gaps, contradictions, and unanswered questions, each with a note on what it would take to move it. The holes in the map are drawn in too, on purpose.

  1. The measurement problem is unresolved. Decoherence (qm-decoherence, E1) explains why we see classical outcomes but not why a single outcome is experienced. Every interpretation — many-worlds (E2), Bohm (E2), GRW/CSL (E2), relational QM (E2), consistent histories (E2), Copenhagen (E2) — reproduces the same statistics (measurement-problem-statement, E1). What would move it: an experiment distinguishing the interpretations — most concretely, an objective-collapse deviation from unitary QM, which GRW/CSL is actively being constrained against.
  1. There is no confirmed physical substrate of experience. COGITATE 2025 (cogitate-adversarial-collaboration-2025, E1 as a study) vindicated neither IIT (iit-integrated-information) nor GNWT (gnwt-global-workspace) — both were partially disconfirmed, and both remain disputed. Orch-OR (orch-or-microtubules) still faces Tegmark's decoherence-timescale objection; the 2025 microtubule-anesthesia work (wiest-anesthesia-microtubule-observation) is suggestive, not confirmatory, and leans partly on a contested entanglement claim. What would move it: direct evidence of functionally relevant quantum coherence in neurons, or a theory whose neural predictions survive the next adversarial test.
  1. The decombination problem. Cosmopsychism and the O-Source (cosmopsychism, E4), analytic idealism (analytic-idealism-kastrup, E4), and the experience-first metaphysics carried by the Nameless project (nameless-experience-first-metaphysics, E4) must all explain how one cosmic subject or experiential ground yields many bounded private subjects without simply reintroducing the hard problem. Kastrup's dissociation analogy arguably relocates rather than solves it, and the other views inherit the same gap. This is the mirror of panpsychism's combination problem (panpsychism-combination-problem). What would move it: a rigorous account of how one subject individuates into many — but this is philosophy (E4), not something evidence can settle.
  1. The quantum-to-consciousness bridge rests on a single, weak claim. The only claim in the corpus that literally makes a mind collapse the wavefunction is von Neumann–Wigner (von-neumann-wigner-consciousness-collapse, E3, disputed). Decoherence independently dissolves its original motivation; the quantum Zeno effect already falsifies its naive versions (Chalmers & McQueen 2022); and de Barros & Oas (2017) argue that its surviving versions are structurally near-unfalsifiable. The physics "observer" is a measurement interaction, not a mind (the observer-dependence bridge, ceiling E3). Any move from "observation matters in QM" to "consciousness is fundamental to QM" therefore hangs on this one disputed E3 claim, and must be labeled as such. What would move it: a detected consciousness-dependent effect on a quantum outcome — none has ever been observed.
  1. Observation is not interpretation — a distinction that recurs. The corpus deliberately splits each finding from its metaphysical reading, and the gap is real in every case: - AI: the bliss attractor (claude-spiritual-bliss-attractor-observation, E3) and concept-injection introspection (anthropic-introspective-awareness-concept-injection, E3) are output and activation statistics; the sentience and introspection readings (spiritual-bliss-machine-sentience-interpretation, llm-introspection-genuine-awareness-interpretation, both E4) are equally well explained by the RLHF-persona account. No validated marker of machine experience exists. - NDEs: AWARE-II lucid recall (aware-ii-nde-phenomenology, E3) had a near-chance objective test (1 of 28); the survival and non-local reading (aware-ii-nde-survival-interpretation, E4) goes well beyond it. - Terminal lucidity: the reports are real (terminal-lucidity-observation, E3); the filter reading (terminal-lucidity-filter-interpretation, E4) makes no distinct testable prediction — "the filter loosened" and "spared circuits transiently recovered" predict the same data. What would move each: an observable that the interpretation predicts and its materialist rival does not.
  1. Illusionism is the sharpest rival to the whole family. illusionism-eliminativism (E4) holds that there is no phenomenal consciousness to explain, only a robust representation of one. If it is right, the hard problem dissolves and the entire consciousness-is-fundamental program — including O Theory — loses its motivation. It is not a fringe position; it is the strongest opposing E4. The synthesis must present it as a live rival, not a footnote. What would move it: nothing empirical, since this is argued at E4 — but the unresolved "explain the illusion" challenge is where the debate actually turns.
  1. The traditions do not all agree. "They all point at the O-Source" is the perennial-philosophy common-core thesis (perennial-philosophy-common-core-thesis, E4, disputed), not a fact. Madhyamaka's śūnyatā (madhyamaka-sunyata-emptiness, E5) explicitly resists reification into any "ground of being" or "One" — the tradition declares emptiness itself empty precisely to block that move — so it cannot be folded in with Brahman, the One, and Ein Sof without misrepresentation. Katz's constructivism holds that the experiences are shaped differently all the way down. What would move it: nothing decisive — we have only already-interpreted reports to compare (perennialism-constructivism-debate, E4) — which is exactly why it stays a contested comparative thesis.
  1. The PSR-regress applies to O Theory's own O-Source. The Principle of Sufficient Reason (principle-of-sufficient-reason, E4) demands a reason for every fact — including the O-Source itself. If the O-Source needs no explanation, then so might the physical world (no O-Source required); if it does need one, the regress is not stopped. This objection is filed against O Theory's own ground, not only against rival substrates. The same fairness applies to the Nameless project's "nothing must distinguish itself" argument (nameless-experience-first-metaphysics, E4): that we cannot conceive an undifferentiated void without importing a contrast shows a limit of representation, not that reality's nothing is compelled to become something.
  1. Psychometric circularity in mysticism. The MEQ30 (meq30-mystical-experience-psychometrics) and the M-Scale (hood-mysticism-scale-psychometrics) are real, replicated E3 instruments — but their items were written from Stace's categories (stace-common-core-typology, E5), so finding "Stace-shaped" experiences partly reflects the questionnaire's own design. The DMN correlations (meditation-dmn-neuroscience, psychedelic-ego-dissolution-dmn) are cross-sectional and correlational, with self-selection confounds, and the psilocybin RCTs (griffiths-psilocybin-mystical-experience) suffer functional unblinding. What would move it: instruments derived independently of Stace's typology, and blinded or longitudinal designs.
  1. The 2025 LLM observations have not been independently replicated. Both the bliss attractor and the concept-injection results are single-lab (Anthropic) and unreplicated. Until a non-Anthropic group reproduces them, they are provisional even as observations. What would move it: independent replication of the feature-steering and concept-detection effects.
  1. The free-energy principle's unfalsifiability. In its most general form the FEP (predictive-processing-free-energy-principle, E2, disputed) faces the "dark room" objection: if organisms minimize prediction error, why not seek a dark, silent, unchanging room? A framework that can absorb any behavior is unfalsifiable. What would move it: a specification of the principle that forbids some observable behavior.
  1. The competing metaphysics are rivals, not complements. Information-first (it-from-bit-information-ontology, pancomputationalism-digital-physics, the MUH), process-first (process-philosophy-actual-occasions), structure-first (ontic-structural-realism), and Platonism (mathematical-platonism) are mutually exclusive answers to "what is fundamental." O Theory cannot lean on all of them at once, and the synthesis must not present them as a harmonious stack when they are competitors — each with its own decisive objection (Newman's problem, the description-versus-thing-described conflation, Benacerraf's access problem).
  1. A family of "seed" theories of everything — real mathematics, unlicensed conclusions, mostly unreviewed. A recognizable family of recent proposals tries to grow all of physics from a single geometric or informational seed, and they are weighed here evenly, with no member singled out: Lisi's E8 model (lisi-e8-toe, E3) from an exceptional Lie group; Nielsen's Topological Unified Field Theory (nielsen-topological-unified-field-theory, E3) from a complex Hopf fibration; the Nameless project (nurecas.com) (nameless-qubit-standard-model-derivation, E3) from a single qubit's geometry; and, failing vetting outright, IHC (ihc-inverted-hypersphere-cosmology, E6), Observer Patch Holography (observer-patch-holography-toe, E6), and Haramein's holographic-mass model (haramein-schwarzschild-proton-holographic-mass, E6). Two tensions run through the whole family. First, the underlying mathematics is often genuinely E1 — the Hopf fibration and the CP∞ bundle classification are settled topology (hopf-fibration-topology-physics, u1-bundles-classified-by-cp-infinity) — but the unification conclusion does not follow from it: the classifying-space theorem constrains only abelian U(1) bundles and privileges no particular finite stage, so deriving a specific spacetime, the full non-abelian Standard Model, and gravity is an unlicensed step. Second, matching small-integer or golden-ratio values (sin²θ_W = 3/8 is the known SU(5) value; 4/13 ≈ 0.3077; golden-ratio fits to α) risks numerology — with enough freedom in how modes map to particle content, there are many simple expressions near any target, so a fit is not a mechanism. These works are largely self-published (viXra, Zenodo, GitHub, a young non-Scopus journal) and not independently reviewed, which is why the credentialed ones sit at E3 and the rest at E6 — the line is drawn by author and venue, not by confirmation, and none is confirmed. What would move it: independent physicists checking the algebra (does it force the gauge content, or reverse-engineer it?), a refereed derivation of a not-yet-measured quantity, or an experiment — for example, JUNO deciding a predicted neutrino-mixing value around 2030 (see predictions.md), which is one such test among the family, not a verdict on the whole.
  1. The standing cross-register temptation. The strongest pull in this whole area is to let an E5 metaphor ("all is one"), an E3 observation ("microtubules!", "the model introspects!"), an E3 unification proposal ("physics from a single seed!"), or an E2 result ("information reconstructs QM!") do E1 work. This ledger exists partly to keep naming that pull. The register-and-ceiling label on every bridge, the observation-versus-interpretation split, and the deliberate separation of genuine E1 mathematics from the E3 conclusions drawn from it are the mechanisms that catch it before publish.
