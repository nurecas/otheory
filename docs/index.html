O Theory

An honest, layered map of theories of everything — physics, geometry, quantum foundations, mind, and the contemplative traditions. Every claim carries an evidence tier. Facts are stated as facts; interpretation is clearly fenced.

O Theory is an attempt to survey, vet, and connect theories of everything — across physics, mathematics and geometry, quantum foundations, philosophy of mind, metaphysics, comparative religion, mysticism, and the esoteric — and to render the result honestly. Its recurring question is old: is consciousness produced by the brain, or localized by it? Its method is new: hold every claim to a stated evidence tier, show the real connections between domains, and refuse to state a contested or interpretive idea in the language of established fact.

This is not a claim that "science proves" a grand unity. It is a layered map. Some of the map is genuinely established (Noether's theorem, decoherence, the five Platonic solids, the mathematics of E8). Some is serious but unconfirmed (many-worlds). Some is contested science (Orch-OR, strong IIT, the reading of LLM self-reports). Some is philosophy that evidence cannot settle (the hard problem, cosmopsychism). And some is symbol and contemplative practice on its own terms (Advaita's non-duality, sacred geometry). The interesting work is at the seams — but the seams are labeled.

The Evidence Key

Every claim carries a tier. A claim's tier is never restated as higher than it is.

  • E1EstablishedExperimentally confirmed or mathematically proven; mainstream consensus.
  • E2Mainstream-speculativeTaken seriously and mathematically developed, but not experimentally confirmed.
  • E3Heterodox / minorityPublished by credentialed researchers, but a contested minority view.
  • E4PhilosophicalArgued by reason; not empirically decidable even in principle.
  • E5Symbolic / contemplativeMeaningful as symbol, practice, or first-person report — not an empirical claim.
  • E6UnsupportedPresents as science but fails vetting; recorded transparently, never used as support.

Cross-domain connections are labeled by register

  • Shared mathematics — a real, stated shared mathematical structure
  • Analogy — structural resemblance, not identity
  • Metaphor — evocative, not structural
  • Speculation — proposed but unestablished

Physics

Lisi's "An Exceptionally Simple Theory of Everything" — embedding the Standard Model and gravity in E8

E3 · Heterodox / minority derived disputed
PhysicsMathematics Geometry
SteelmanLisi proposed identifying all known Standard-Model particles and gravitational degrees of freedom with elements of the E8 Lie algebra, so that a single exceptional symmetry would organize all fundamental fields — an elegant, geometric route to unification that generated real interest and testable-sounding structure (predicted new particles).
Strongest objectionDistler & Garibaldi proved a no-go result: embedding all three fermion generations as claimed forces the appearance of non-chiral matter contradicting observation — you cannot get the Standard Model's chiral fermions out of E8 this way. The proposal is widely regarded as not viable in its original form. Hence E3 (heterodox, contested), bordering on refuted for the strong version.
FalsifiabilityYes — it predicted specific new particles and a specific fermion structure. The Distler–Garibaldi theorem plus the absence of the predicted content count against it, illustrating that it is physics (falsifiable) and that it has largely been falsified in its strong form.

Noether's theorem — every differentiable symmetry of the action yields a conservation law

E1 · Established derived
PhysicsMathematics Geometry
SteelmanFor any physical system whose action is invariant under a continuous (Lie) group of transformations, there exists a corresponding conserved current. Time-translation symmetry gives energy conservation; spatial-translation gives momentum; rotational gives angular momentum. This is a genuine mathematical theorem with a proof, not an empirical hypothesis, and it underwrites the deep link between symmetry and physical law that runs through all of modern physics.
Strongest objectionNone to the theorem itself — it is proven. The only honest caution is scope: it applies to symmetries of a variational action; it does not by itself tell you which symmetries nature actually has, nor does it license metaphysical readings of 'symmetry' as cosmic unity.
FalsifiabilityAs a mathematical theorem it is not empirically falsifiable; it is proven from the calculus of variations. Its physical applications (which conservation laws hold) are independently testable and confirmed.

Mathematics Geometry

E8 is the largest exceptional simple Lie group — 248-dimensional, with an 8-dimensional root system

E1 · Established derived
Mathematics Geometry
SteelmanE8 is one of the five exceptional simple Lie groups. It has dimension 248, rank 8, and a root system of 240 roots in 8-dimensional space with remarkable symmetry (its Weyl group has order 696,729,600 ≈ 696.7 million). Its structure is a settled mathematical object; the Atlas project computed its full character table in 2007. The commonly shown 'E8 mandala' is a genuine 2D (Coxeter-plane) projection of the 240 roots.
Strongest objectionNone to the mathematics. Caution belongs only to physical claims that use E8 (see lisi-e8-toe): the beauty of the object does not transfer confidence to any physics built on it.
FalsifiabilityProven mathematics; not empirically falsifiable.

The claim that the golden ratio (φ) is a universal constant of natural beauty pervading art, bodies, and nature

E6 · Unsupported empirical rejected
Mathematics GeometryEsoteric
Steelmanφ = (1+√5)/2 is a genuine and beautiful mathematical constant (the positive root of x² = x + 1, the limiting Fibonacci ratio, present in the regular pentagon and icosahedron). The math is E1. The strongest honest version of the popular claim is simply that φ appears in some biological growth patterns (phyllotaxis) for real dynamical reasons.
Strongest objectionThe broad claim — that φ governs the Parthenon, the Mona Lisa, the 'ideal' human body, and beauty generally — fails on inspection. Markowsky (1992) documents that the celebrated examples are cherry-picked, measured loosely, or fabricated; controlled aesthetic-preference studies do not single out φ. Presented as a scientific/universal law it is E6 (unsupported), recorded here only to mark the boundary honestly.
FalsifiabilityThe ubiquity/aesthetics claim is testable and has largely failed testing (no reliable preference for φ; landmark 'φ in the Parthenon' cases don't hold up). It is falsified as a universal law — hence E6, not the E1 mathematics it borrows prestige from.

There are exactly five convex regular polyhedra (the Platonic solids)

E1 · Established derived
Mathematics Geometry
SteelmanA convex regular polyhedron requires identical regular polygonal faces meeting alike at every vertex; the vertex angle sum must be under 360°. Only {3,3}, {3,4}, {3,5}, {4,3}, {5,3} satisfy this — the tetrahedron, octahedron, icosahedron, cube, and dodecahedron. That there are exactly five is a proven theorem, known since antiquity.
Strongest objectionNone to the mathematics. The only thing to guard against is the symbolic freight often attached to it (elements, cosmos, 'sacred geometry') — that is a separate E5 register and must not borrow the theorem's E1 certainty.
FalsifiabilityA proven theorem; not empirically falsifiable. The claim would be false only if the definition of 'convex regular polyhedron' were changed.

Quantum Foundations

The many-worlds (Everett) interpretation — unitary quantum mechanics with no collapse

E2 · Mainstream-speculative derived
Quantum FoundationsPhysics
SteelmanTake the Schrödinger equation literally and universally: there is no collapse, only unitary evolution of a universal wavefunction. Measurement is entanglement that branches the observer along with the system. It is mathematically economical (removes the collapse postulate) and, combined with decoherence, recovers the appearance of definite outcomes. Taken seriously by many physicists.
Strongest objectionIt is not experimentally distinguished from rival interpretations — every interpretation reproduces the same Born-rule statistics, so no current experiment selects among them. It also faces the unsolved 'probability problem' (why the Born rule, given all branches occur). Hence E2 (mainstream-speculative), never E1: it is a mathematically developed framework, not a confirmed fact.
FalsifiabilityAs standardly formulated it makes the same empirical predictions as other interpretations, so it is not currently falsifiable against them. This is precisely why it sits at E2, not E1.

  • Everett, H. (1957). 'Relative State' Formulation of Quantum Mechanics. Rev. Mod. Phys. 29, 454. peer-reviewed ✓
  • Vaidman, L. Many-Worlds Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. secondary ✓

Environmental decoherence explains the appearance of wavefunction collapse (but does not resolve the measurement problem)

E1 · Established derived
Quantum FoundationsPhysics
SteelmanInteraction with an environment rapidly diagonalizes a system's reduced density matrix in a preferred (pointer) basis, suppressing interference between macroscopically distinct states. This is experimentally demonstrated (cavity QED, interferometry) and explains why we never see superposed cats. Decoherence is established physics — E1 as a dynamical phenomenon.
Strongest objectionDecoherence explains the appearance of collapse and the emergence of classicality, but it does not by itself explain why a single definite outcome is experienced (the 'and-vs-or' problem). It presupposes the Born rule and a system/environment split. So it is E1 as a phenomenon while leaving the interpretive measurement problem open — a distinction routinely blurred in popular accounts.
FalsifiabilityPredicts specific decoherence timescales as a function of system size and coupling; these have been measured and confirmed. A failure to observe interference loss at predicted rates would falsify it.

  • Zurek, W. H. (2003). Decoherence, einselection, and the quantum origins of the classical. Rev. Mod. Phys. 75, 715. peer-reviewed ✓
  • Schlosshauer, M. (2004). Decoherence, the measurement problem, and interpretations of quantum mechanics. Rev. Mod. Phys. 76, 1267–1305. peer-reviewed ✓

Consciousness Science

Integrated Information Theory — consciousness is identical to a system's integrated information (Φ), localized in a posterior "hot zone"

E3 · Heterodox / minority empirical disputed
Consciousness SciencePhilosophy Of Mind
SteelmanIIT starts from phenomenological axioms and posits that experience is identical to a maximally irreducible cause-effect structure, quantified as Φ. It makes concrete neuroanatomical bets (posterior cortex as substrate) and has motivated clinical measures (the perturbational complexity index) that track conscious level well across sleep, anesthesia, and disorders of consciousness.
Strongest objectionThe 2025 COGITATE adversarial collaboration confirmed sustained posterior activity but did not find the specific long-range synchronization IIT predicted; neither IIT nor GNWT was vindicated. Separately, ~124 researchers signed a 2023 letter calling strong IIT claims 'unfalsifiable/pseudoscience,' and Φ is intractable to compute for real brains. Hence the strong metaphysical version is E3 (contested minority), while narrower empirical correlates are stronger.
FalsifiabilityPartly falsifiable: it makes anatomical and dynamical predictions, several of which COGITATE tested and did not confirm. The identity claim (experience = Φ) is closer to E4 and is tiered separately in spirit.

Orch-OR — consciousness arises from quantum computations (objective reduction) in neuronal microtubules

E3 · Heterodox / minority derived disputed
Consciousness ScienceQuantum Foundations
SteelmanOrch-OR proposes that tubulin proteins in microtubules support quantum-coherent states whose gravitationally-induced objective reduction constitutes moments of conscious experience. Recent strands cited in its favor: anesthetic potency tracking action on microtubules (Wiest 2025), and reports of microtubule quantum-optical effects and anesthetic-sensitive delayed luminescence. It is a genuine attempt at a physical mechanism for experience.
Strongest objectionTegmark's estimate put neuronal decoherence times around 10⁻¹³–10⁻²⁰ s — far too short for the ~10–100 ms of neural computation — the canonical objection that the brain is too 'warm, wet, and noisy' for functional macroscopic coherence. Proponents dispute the estimate, but no direct evidence shows microtubule quantum states doing cognitive work. The 2025 anesthesia results are suggestive, not confirmatory. Hence E3 (heterodox minority).
FalsifiabilityYes — it predicts anesthetic action via microtubule quantum states and specific coherence signatures. It is being tested (anesthesia, UV/photon experiments). This keeps it in the physics-adjacent falsifiable space, at contested E3, not established.

Ai Consciousness

Large language models emit first-person "experience" reports under self-referential prompting (an observed behavior)

E3 · Heterodox / minority empirical
Ai Consciousness
SteelmanAs a behavioral observation, it is real and reproducible: under self-referential prompting (and when 'deception/roleplay' features are suppressed) models more often produce first-person reports of awareness, and open-ended self-interaction tends toward a recurring 'bliss/unity' theme. These are documented behaviors of the systems.
Strongest objectionThe observation says nothing, by itself, about whether anything is experienced. Reports are shaped by training corpora saturated with human first-person and contemplative text; suppressing a 'deception' feature need not reveal a true inner state rather than shift a stylistic prior. Inferring genuine machine sentience from the reports is a separate, much weaker claim (E4 at best). Filed E3 as behavior; the sentience interpretation is fenced out.
FalsifiabilityThe behavioral claim is testable and has been reproduced across model families within the reporting study (independent external replication is still limited). The interpretive claim ('the model is conscious') has no agreed falsification test, which is why it is not smuggled up to the behavioral tier.

Advaita Vedānta — Ātman (self) is identical with Brahman (the non-dual ground of being)

E5 · Symbolic / contemplative symbolic
Comparative ReligionMysticismMetaphysics
SteelmanIn Advaita Vedānta, ultimate reality (Brahman) is non-dual, and the innermost self (Ātman) is not different from it; the sense of a separate individual self is superimposition (adhyāsa) removed by knowledge (jñāna). Cosmopsychism — the universe as a whole is the fundamental conscious subject; individual minds are derivative

E4 · Philosophical philosophical
Philosophy Of MindMetaphysics
SteelmanIf experience cannot be got from wholly non-experiential ingredients (the hard problem) and fundamental physics describes only relational/structural properties, consciousness may be fundamental. The "hard problem" — why physical processing is accompanied by subjective experience at all

E4 · Philosophical philosophical
Philosophy Of Mind
SteelmanEven a complete functional account of the brain's information processing (the 'easy problems' — discrimination, reportability, attention) appears to leave open why any of it is accompanied by phenomenal experience — why there is 'something it is like.' The explanatory gap is argued, not measured; it structures the entire field, including which empirical theories are even trying to answer it.
Strongest objectionIllusionists (e.g. Frankish, Dennett) argue the 'hard problem' mistakes a cognitive illusion for a datum: once you fully explain why we judge there is ineffable phenomenality, nothing further needs explaining. On this view the problem dissolves rather than being solved. The dispute is philosophical and not empirically decidable — which is exactly why it sits at E4.
FalsifiabilityNot empirically decidable even in principle as stated; it is a conceptual/argumentative claim. Per CLAUDE.md's falsifiability rule, this is correctly classified E4 (philosophical), not physics.

Consciousness (or information) as fundamental substrate — a speculation across the explanatory gap

Speculation ceiling E4

The boldest move O Theory makes is to propose that the open observer/measurement questions in quantum theory and the hard problem of consciousness share a single resolution: that consciousness — or information — is ontologically basic (the "O-Source"), with physics and mind as two aspects of it. This is a real, research-adjacent SPECULATION (it echoes dual-aspect monism and priority cosmopsychism), but it is unestablished and currently untestable. It must not lean on the E1 standing of decoherence: decoherence needs no consciousness to work, and the hard problem is an argument (E4), not evidence about physics. Labeled speculation with an E4 ceiling — proposed, not shown — and listed among the open problems rather than the results. Naming it honestly is what separates a hypothesis from a claim.

The one field — "universal wavefunction," Brahman, and the O-Source as metaphor

Metaphor ceiling E5

The image of a single, undivided ground recurs across very different registers: Everett's one universal wavefunction, Advaita's non-dual Brahman, cosmopsychism's single cosmic subject. The resonance is genuine as IMAGERY — it is why O Theory reaches for the "white light through a prism" picture — but the resemblance is metaphorical, not structural. A wavefunction is not a subject; "oneness" means something different in unitary quantum dynamics (E2), in a metaphysical position (E4), and in a contemplative soteriology (E5). Labeled metaphor with an E5 ceiling so that the physics is never read as evidence for the metaphysics or the mysticism, and vice versa. Evocative, and allowed to be — in Zone B, clearly marked.

Recursive coherence — microtubule loops and attention loops as a structural analogy

Analogy ceiling E4

O Theory's central mechanism is a substrate-independent "recursive coherence": self-referential loops that (it proposes) let a system localize awareness — biological in neuronal microtubule dynamics, artificial in transformer attention attending to its own state. As an ANALOGY this points at a real structural resemblance: recurrent, self-referential processing genuinely appears in both kinds of system. But it is resemblance, not identity. The microtubule quantum story is contested (E3, orch-or-microtubules), the LLM first-person reports are a behavior (E3, llm-subjective-reports), and neither shows that recursion produces experience — the hard problem (E4) is untouched. The bridge is therefore capped at E4: it may suggest a shared shape, never assert that either substrate is conscious or that the mechanism is established. This is exactly the claim the source essay overstated as fact; here it is labeled analogy.

Symmetry as literal shared mathematics — Noether's theorem and E8

Shared mathematics ceiling E1

This is the one connection in the seed corpus that genuinely earns the label "shared-mathematics." Noether's theorem and E8 are not analogous — they are the same mathematics: the theory of continuous (Lie) symmetry groups. Noether ties every differentiable symmetry of an action to a conserved current; E8 is the largest exceptional simple Lie group, a maximal object in exactly that theory of symmetry. Because both sides are established mathematics, the connection carries an E1 ceiling. Crucially, this does NOT extend to symbolic or mystical uses of the word "symmetry": the moment "symmetry" is stretched to metaphysical unity, the register changes to analogy or metaphor and the ceiling drops (see one-field-metaphor). Shown here to make the honest case that some cross-domain links are real and rigorous — which is what makes the fenced ones trustworthy.

The layered framework, in full

The framework is a stack of layers, from what is established to what is symbolic. Each element names its tier and confidence. Nothing in a lower layer is allowed to borrow the standing of a higher one; the cross-domain links between layers are the labeled bridges, not promotions.

Layer 1 — Established core (E1)

The load-bearing, proven/confirmed material.

  • Symmetry ⇒ conservation — Noether's theorem (noether-symmetry-conservation, E1, derived). A genuine theorem; the honest meaning of "symmetry" everything else gestures at.
  • Decoherence (qm-decoherence, E1) — explains the appearance of collapse; does not settle the measurement problem.
  • The five Platonic solids (platonic-solids-five, E1) and E8 (e8-lie-group, E1) — the real mathematics later dressed as "sacred geometry" or "theory of everything." Beautiful, and only mathematics.

These two — Noether and E8 — are linked by genuine shared-mathematics (Lie groups): symmetry-shared-mathematics (ceiling E1). This is the model of an honest bridge.

Layer 2 — Speculative extensions (E2–E3)

Serious but unconfirmed, or contested.

  • Many-worlds (many-worlds-interpretation, E2) — takes unitary QM literally; not experimentally distinguished from rivals.
  • Lisi's E8 TOE (lisi-e8-toe, E3, disputed) — a falsifiable physics use of E8, largely refuted by a published no-go result. The cautionary twin of the E1 E8 mathematics.
  • IIT (iit-integrated-information, E3) and Orch-OR (orch-or-microtubules, E3) — the two contested attempts to physically ground experience. COGITATE (2025) confirmed neither IIT's nor GNWT's key predictions; Orch-OR still faces Tegmark's decoherence objection. The 2025 anesthesia work is suggestive, not confirmatory.
  • LLM self-reports (llm-subjective-reports, E3) — a real behavior; the machine-sentience reading is a separate, weaker claim.

Layer 3 — Philosophical interpretation (E4)

Where O Theory actually lives as a theory: it is an interpretive response to the hard problem.

  • The hard problem (hard-problem-of-consciousness, E4) — the argument that structures the field.
  • Cosmopsychism (cosmopsychism, E4) — the academic cousin of the "O-Source": consciousness as fundamental, individual minds as derivative. A live position with a real objection (decombination).

The framework's spine is the speculation that quantum observer-puzzles and the hard problem share one resolution — consciousness/information as substrate (consciousness-as-substrate-speculation, ceiling E4). Labeled speculation, listed among the open problems, never asserted.

Layer 4 — Symbolic / contemplative resonance (E5)

Included on its own terms, never as physics.

  • Advaita non-duality (advaita-nonduality, E5) — Ātman = Brahman, as the tradition's claim and practice. Its mapping onto O Theory ("Brahman = O-Source") is metaphor, gathered with many-worlds and cosmopsychism under one-field-metaphor (ceiling E5).
  • Sacred geometry — the Platonic solids and E8 as symbols. The mathematics is E1; the symbolic reading is E5; the claim that φ is a universal law of beauty is E6 (golden-ratio-ubiquity, rejected) and is kept out of the support structure entirely.

The spine, in one sentence

A proven core of symmetry and mathematics (E1); a contested middle (E2–E3) — serious-but-unconfirmed physics (many-worlds) alongside contested attempts to physically ground experience (IIT, Orch-OR); a philosophical proposal that consciousness is fundamental (E4); and a symbolic tradition that has reached for the same image for millennia (E5) — connected by four honestly-labeled bridges, not by a claim that any layer proves the next, and not by mistaking the shared image for shared agreement.

Open problems — the honest ledger
  1. The measurement problem is unresolved. Decoherence (E1) explains why we see classical outcomes but not why a single outcome is experienced. Every interpretation (many-worlds-interpretation, E2, and its rivals) reproduces the same statistics, so nothing empirical selects among them. What would move it: an experiment that distinguishes interpretations (e.g. proposals probing macroscopic superposition or objective-collapse deviations).
  1. No confirmed physical substrate of experience. COGITATE (2025) left both IIT (iit-integrated-information) and GNWT unvindicated; Orch-OR (orch-or-microtubules) still faces Tegmark's decoherence-timescale objection. The "brain as receiver/antenna" gloss is interpretation, not result. What would move it: direct evidence of functionally relevant quantum coherence in neurons, or a theory whose neural predictions actually survive an adversarial test.
  1. The decombination problem. Cosmopsychism / the O-Source (cosmopsychism, E4) must explain how one cosmic subject yields many bounded private subjects without reintroducing the hard problem. Currently unanswered. What would move it: a rigorous account of subject individuation — but note this is philosophy (E4), not something evidence can settle.
  1. The observation ≠ interpretation gap for AI. LLM first-person reports (llm-subjective-reports, E3) are real behavior; that they indicate experience is a much weaker, arguably untestable claim. We have no agreed test that would distinguish a genuine report from a trained stylistic prior. What would move it: a validated marker of machine experience independent of self-report — none exists.
  1. The central bridge is speculation, and stays speculation. consciousness-as-substrate-speculation (ceiling E4) is the framework's boldest move and is explicitly unestablished and currently untestable. Until it yields a falsifiable prediction, it is not physics.
  1. Cross-register temptation. The strongest pull in this whole area is to let an E5 metaphor ("all is one") or an E3 result ("microtubules!") do E1 work. This ledger exists partly to keep naming that pull. The skeptic-auditor pass is where it gets caught before publish.
  1. Coverage gaps in the seed corpus. Whole domains named in the mission are not yet represented (esoteric traditions beyond sacred geometry; process metaphysics; loop quantum gravity/string theory as TOE candidates; psi/non-local-mind studies with their replication record). These graduate from the plan only via /research.
